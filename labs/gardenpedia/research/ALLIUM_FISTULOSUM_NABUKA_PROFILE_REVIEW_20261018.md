# Evergreen Bunching Onion Nabuka — profile enrichment review (2026-10-18)

## Scope and evidence

- Kew POWO accepts *Allium fistulosum*: `kew-allium-fistulosum-500` (taxonomy/identity only).
- UMN Extension identifies scallions/bunching onions as *A. fistulosum*, distinguishes them from Egyptian walking onion (*A. cepa* var. *proliferum*), describes the non-bulbing stalk form, and gives outdoor watering, early weed-control, and whole-plant harvest guidance: `umn-growing-scallions-500`.
- OSU Extension's bunching-onion guide supports crop-type cultivation and repeat leaf cutting/regrowth during the growing season: `osu-bunching-onions-2022`. This is not a maturity or regrowth guarantee for a named seed lot.

## Editorial changes

Added bilingual identity and care sections to the existing profile. The identity text explicitly limits the evidence to species/crop level and does not claim that these sources verify a cultivar named “Nabuka”. Care focuses on gentle early weed control. Existing OSU-backed regrowth wording was retained: OSU explicitly supports repeat leaf regrowth for bunching onions. UMN's separate second-season cut-and-regrow guidance is for Egyptian walking onion and was not applied to *A. fistulosum*.

No new source record, taxonomy, compatibility value, spacing value, or private seed-inventory detail was added. The profile remains partial pending stronger identity-specific evidence and editorial review.
