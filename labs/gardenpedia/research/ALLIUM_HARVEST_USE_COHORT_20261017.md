# Allium Harvest Use cohort — 2026-10-17

## Scope

Added bilingual Harvest Use records for three existing identities:

- `garlic-allium-sativum` — species-level garlic, hardneck/softneck type not selected.
- `seed-grown-shallot` — seed-grown shallot crop type, no named cultivar.
- `tokyo-long-white-onion` — Tokyo Long White bunching onion, `Allium fistulosum`.

No identity or Plant Profile was replaced, and no editorial status was promoted.

## Evidence choices

- USU Extension garlic garden guidance supports harvest/cure cues; its garlic
  food guide supports clove preparation. UMN Extension supports the conditional
  scape guidance only for garlic that actually forms a hardneck scape.
- USU Extension's shallot guide explicitly covers both seed- and set-grown
  shallots, green-leaf harvest, mature-bulb harvest, curing and storage. Its
  Utah rates and schedules were not generalized.
- Oregon State lists Tokyo Long White among `A. fistulosum` bunching types;
  UMN Extension supplies species/crop-level scallion harvest and culinary uses.
  Those uses are not presented as unique flavor or performance traits of this
  named type.

## Boundaries retained

- Garlic scapes are shown only when a hardneck plant forms one; no garlic type
  is assigned to the species record.
- Shallot harvest is separated into green-leaf and mature-bulb stages. No
  cultivar flavor, recipe, or storage-life guarantee was added.
- Tokyo Long White has the ordinary crop-level scallion use profile. No exact
  cultivar harvest day, size, or system compatibility is inferred.
