## Deferred identity issue

`lemon-thyme` was not rewritten in this cohort. Its current label uses
*Thymus citriodorus*, while Kew POWO accepts *Thymus × citriodorus* and treats
it as a hybrid. The accepted-name / Garden identity relationship needs an
explicit reconciliation decision before changing the canonical scientific
name. This is not resolved by applying information from the distinct RHS
selection 'Culinary Lemon'.

## Editorial disposition

All three reviewed profiles remain `partial`: the new evidence improves useful
coverage but does not establish all material cultivation and evidence questions
for every use context. The inventory status was updated; the structural
section-gap matrix was not treated as an editorial completion ledger.

## Empress of India scope disposition — 2026-10-19

RHS identifies the named selection as *Tropaeolum majus* 'Empress of India' in
one profile and as genus-level *Tropaeolum* 'Empress of India' in another;
Johnny's lists the same named seed selection as *T. minus*. The species conflict
remains in the taxonomy reconciliation record. The public Plant Profile treats
the selection at genus scope, retains its applicable bilingual ornamental
guidance, and makes no species-dependent edible-use claim. Its Plant Profile
disposition is complete at genus/selection scope; that does not mark the species
assignment resolved.
