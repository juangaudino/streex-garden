# Bean and pea Harvest Use cohort — 2026-10-18

## Scope

Added bilingual Harvest Use entries for nine existing identities: Blue Lake bush
bean, Royal Burgundy bush bean, Henderson bush lima bean, Kentucky Wonder pole
bean, purple-podded common bean, Little Marvel shelling pea, Mammoth Melting Sugar
snow pea, Oregon Sugar Pod II snow pea, and the sugar-snap pea type. No identities,
Seed Profiles, or source records were added. All nine Plant Profiles remain
`partial`; a Harvest Use entry alone is not a full profile audit.

## Evidence and taxonomic scope

- University of Minnesota Extension, [Growing beans in home gardens](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-beans): common-bean snap, shell and dry harvest stages; bush versus pole habit and repeat harvest; lima-specific fresh-shell and dry harvest; purple pod color changing to dark green during cooking; and food-safety cautions. These crop/type statements were not turned into cultivar-specific dates, yields, recipes, or dimensions.
- University of Minnesota Extension, [Growing peas in home gardens](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-peas): distinguishes shelling, snow and sugar-snap peas; their different harvest cues; rapid quality decline after maturity; cooling and freezing as use/handling options. Oregon Sugar Pod II and Mammoth Melting Sugar are kept in the snow-pea class; neither is assigned the sugar-snap harvest cue.
- The existing Johnny's Royal Burgundy product source is used only for the named product identity/purple pod characteristic. No product-page agronomy was generalized to common beans.

## Editorial correction

The `purple-podded-bean` growth section incorrectly described fava bean
(*Vicia faba*) traits even though the identity is a purple-podded bush type of
common bean (*Phaseolus vulgaris*). It now uses UMN's bush-bean guidance (upright,
generally unsupported, about two feet) explicitly at crop-type scope, not as a
measured cultivar specification. The correction is mirrored in the Spanish
translation. Fava's cool-season identity remains distinct.

## Boundaries and checks

- Lima beans remain *Phaseolus lunatus*, separate from common bean; raw lima
  consumption is explicitly discouraged.
- Shelling peas are distinguished from edible-pod snow and sugar-snap peas.
- Food preservation notes refer users to current tested instructions; no canning,
  pickling, blanching, or processing recipe was invented.
- The 501-identity inventory marks Harvest Use present for these nine, without
  promoting their overall completion status.
- Content validator: 506 loaded plant records, 1,009 sources, 328 Seed Profiles,
  zero unresolved source IDs, and 458 taxonomy records. Publisher prepared 501
  public varieties.
