# Beet profile and Harvest Use cohort — 2026-10-17

## Scope

Reviewed `beet-detroit-dark-red`, `golden-beet`, and `cylindra-beet`. `chioggia-beet` was inspected and deliberately left unchanged because it already has a cultivar-specific profile with supported identity, root appearance, crop context, harvest, care, and problems. Existing work on `beet-detroit-dark-red` was preserved and extended rather than replaced.

## Evidence used and scope

- Utah State University Extension, [How to Grow Beets in Your Garden](https://extension.usu.edu/yardandgarden/research/beets-in-the-garden): outdoor table-beet crop guidance on roots and greens, moisture, heat, harvest, and common problems. Its field distances are not indoor pod spacing.
- Mississippi State University Extension Service, [Beets](https://www.extension.msstate.edu/lawn-and-garden/vegetable-gardens/beets): variety/type descriptions. It describes Detroit Dark Red roots as globular, smooth, uniform, and deep red; Cylindra as a long cylindrical beet with uniform slices; and Golden Beet as orange-skinned with yellow interior. These facts do not establish full plant dimensions, yield, or hydroponic fit.
- Utah State University Extension, [Fruit and Vegetable Guide Series: Beets](https://extension.usu.edu/nutrition/research/beets): crop-level fresh and cooked uses, greens, preparation, and household refrigeration. The source says color groups have virtually the same taste; no Golden-specific flavor claim was made. The household storage period is qualified as an estimate. No canning process was copied from this overview.

## Changes

- Replaced the three wave-baseline summaries with type-appropriate information and named the evidence scope in the affected identity/growth sections.
- Added a bilingual Harvest Use record for Golden Beet and Cylindra Beet, and extended the existing Detroit Dark Red record with a general raw/cooked preparation method. Culinary instructions remain crop-level unless a source explicitly describes a named type.
- Added source records for Mississippi State Extension and the USU culinary guide.
- Kept hydroponic suitability, mature plant spread, and universal system spacing unknown; a root-shape/length descriptor is not a whole-plant size measurement.

## Not established / retained as unknown

- No cultivar-independent hydroponic suitability, EC/pH, indoor light setting, Garden pod spacing, or machine fit was established.
- Golden Beet is retained as a broad color/type identity, not silently resolved to a particular named cultivar.
- No precise cooking times for these identities, tested pickling/canning procedure, flavor superiority, or guaranteed refrigerator life was inferred.
- All three profiles remain `partial`; this cohort review is not a complete individual audit of their sources, translations, companions, visual guides, and every profile layer.
