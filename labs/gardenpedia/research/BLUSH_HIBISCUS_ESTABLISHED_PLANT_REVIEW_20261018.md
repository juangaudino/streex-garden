# Blush Hibiscus established-plant review — 2026-10-18

## Editorial disposition

**Plant Profile: complete.** Review found that the Gardyn Help Center guide
contains substantial established-plant care that had not yet been represented.
The profile now separates those Gardyn-specific instructions from NC State's
outdoor, species-level guidance. The unnamed product selection is not assigned
a cultivar, and Gardyn information is not generalized to other hydroponic
systems.

## Claims added or corrected

- Gardyn's product guide identifies the offered Blush/Red hibiscus as
  *Hibiscus moscheutos*; Kew accepts *Muenchhusia moscheutos* and lists the
  historical name as a synonym. No cultivar denomination was found.
- The product guide lists a Medium Light Zone and approximate 2–3 ft plant size.
  These remain manufacturer/system-specific, not a normalized grow-light tier
  or a cultivar dimension for other conditions.
- Established-system care now includes twice-monthly root checks, trimming
  brown/overlong roots, product-specific topping while retaining two-thirds of
  the plant, the possible bloom delay, post-flowering size management and
  Plant Belt support.
- Flowering now distinguishes NC State's outdoor seasonal description from
  Gardyn's successive-bloom account (individual flowers, batches, rests and
  approximate waves). Gardyn timing is presented as a product report, not a
  universal guarantee.
- Harvest now gives Gardyn's pre-opening cut-flower direction. Its edible
  petals/young-leaf statement is explicitly identified as manufacturer guidance;
  independent food-safety eligibility and treatment history are not verified.
  No broad culinary Harvest Use record was created from that product claim alone.
- Problem cues distinguish Gardyn's product-specific note about some leaf-edge
  or interveinal discoloration from NC State's outdoor dry-soil scorch and
  species-level pest/disease watch list.
- Corrected source attribution so NC State is cited for outdoor recommendations
  and Gardyn for product-specific care. English and Spanish convey the same
  scope and caveats.

## Preserved uncertainty and boundaries

- The product's named cultivar/seed line is not identified.
- No promise is made for a cultivar outside Gardyn, a different hydroponic
  method, or a normalized indoor grow-light requirement.
- NC State presents conflicting fall stem-cleanup advice (cut nearly to ground
  versus leaving 12–24-inch stems for stem-nesting bees); no universal rule is
  selected.
- Independent culinary-safety evidence for this offered product and its
  treatment history remains unavailable; the profile does not endorse eating
  unidentified or treated ornamental plants.
- `compatibilityProfile` and nutrient-engine mappings were not changed.
