# Brassica established-plant cohort — 2026-10-18

## Broccolini

Broccolini remains the unnamed *Brassica oleracea* horticultural type; no
specific seed line/cultivar is represented. OSU and Illinois Extension describe
the broccoli–Chinese broccoli hybrid type. The profile now covers slender
shoot growth, relative (not absolute) heat response, Northern Illinois field
season context, variable repeat harvest, edible shoots and trial-scoped
flea-beetle observation. UCANR/NCSU/OSU sources do not establish dimensions,
storage duration, hydroponic fit or a cultivar-specific harvest size. Its
existing Seed Profile was inspected and left unchanged; pending germination
details remain in the seed-to-plant layer.

## Komatsuna

The published identity remains *Brassica rapa* at species scope. OSU's
infraspecific wording (*B. rapa* var. *perviridis*) is retained as source
terminology, not silently made the accepted Garden taxonomy. OSU describes
dark-green bunching leaves and slender petioles. The commercial Midwest guide
supports a distinction between baby-leaf and full-size production, approximate
group-level harvest timings, bolting risk as days lengthen/warm, and flea
beetle/caterpillar watch items. UCANR's Santa Clara County guidance identifies
open-pollinated Komatsuna as mild/sweet and suitable for salads or cooking, and
describes outer-leaf picking for mustard greens. The production timing and
local flavor recommendation are explicitly not guarantees for every seed line.

Dimensions, exact cultivar, household harvest calendar, indoor light needs,
hydroponic suitability and pod spacing remain unknown. Outdoor commercial soil
spacing is not translated to Garden system positions. The existing Seed
Profile was inspected but not edited.

## Additional Brassica completion follow-up — 2026-10-18

Long Island Improved Brussels Sprouts, Waltham 29 Broccoli, Purple Sprouting
Broccoli, Golden Acre Cabbage, and Georgia Collards passed individual review.

overwintering context from universal climate advice. Purple sprouting broccoli

winter/spring types and newer compact summer/autumn types, without selecting a

placeholder. Waltham 29's supplier-specific traits and crop-level broccoli

not edited.
