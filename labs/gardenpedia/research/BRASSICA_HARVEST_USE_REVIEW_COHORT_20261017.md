# Brassica profile + Harvest Use review cohort — 2026-10-17

## Identities reviewed

Long Island Improved Brussels sprouts, Early White Vienna kohlrabi, Golden Acre
cabbage, Purple of Sicily cauliflower, purple-sprouting broccoli, Red Acre
cabbage, and Waltham 29 broccoli. Existing Plant Profile claims were reviewed
before enrichment; all seven remain **partial** because cultivar dimensions,
hydroponic performance, or other identity-specific evidence is not established.

## Editorial/scope decisions

- Brussels sprouts: bottom-up harvest remains crop-level; the Long Island
  Improved name does not receive unsupported height or timing claims. Illinois
  preparation guidance supports brief cooking and tested freezing practice.
- Kohlrabi: the harvested swollen stem is not described as a root. Illinois
  supports edible stem and leaves and raw/cooked uses; those are not presented as
  Early White Vienna-specific taste claims.
- Golden Acre and Red Acre cabbage: existing Harvest Use records retained.
  General cabbage head formation and handling are not promoted to named-type
  size, maturity, or performance guarantees.
- Purple of Sicily cauliflower: preserve the distinction between white-curd
  blanching and colored curds that need light. White-curd freezing instructions
  were not transferred to the purple type. Culinary methods are crop-level.
- Purple-sprouting broccoli: RHS sprouting-broccoli guidance remains separate
  from calabrese/large-central-head broccoli. Repeated small spears and the
  color change during cooking are presented at the crop/type scope.
- Waltham 29: the supplier's central-head/side-shoot trait remains cultivar
  scoped; general broccoli preparation is not converted into a cultivar flavor
  or storage claim.

## Source conflict and uncertainty

UMN gives Brussels sprouts an approximate refrigerator keeping period of up to
two weeks under its stated bag storage, while Illinois Extension advises using
fresh sprouts within one or two days for best flavor. Harvest Use therefore
encourages prompt use but does not publish a single universal shelf-life.
Outdoor soil spacing, grow-light need, pod spacing and hydroponic compatibility
remain unknown where sources do not establish them.

## Sources added/used

New, narrowly scoped University of Illinois Extension preparation records were
added for Brussels sprouts, kohlrabi, cauliflower, and broccoli. Existing UMN,
Illinois, RHS, and West Coast Seeds sources support the associated crop/type or
cultivar claims at their stated scope. No new identity, API, or canonical
agronomic rule was introduced.
