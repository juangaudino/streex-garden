# Broccolini established-plant profile review — 2026-10-18

## Editorial decision

The public identity remains the unnamed horticultural type `Broccolini` under
`Brassica oleracea`. Oregon State and Illinois Extension describe the crop as a
broccoli–Chinese broccoli (gai lan) cross. No specific seed line or cultivar is
represented, so cultivar dimensions, yield, harvest dates, heat tolerance and
system performance are not assigned. The Plant Profile passed the completion
gate after the bilingual profile was made useful for established, field-grown
plants. This is not a Seed Profile review.

## Claims added or clarified

- **Identity / growth:** long, slender shoots resembling broccoli side shoots;
  multiple shoots and repeated picking are reported, but trial length and
  performance vary among varieties.
- **Cultivation:** Illinois Extension's early-spring / late-summer open-field
  timing and protected fall-season context are explicitly Northern Illinois
  recommendations. Its comparison that broccolini is less likely than broccoli
  to bolt in heat is relative, not immunity. Oregon State's well-drained loam,
  organic matter and even established-soil moisture guidance is labelled
  related broccoli crop guidance, not a Broccolini-specific or indoor recipe.
- **Care / harvest:** follow visible shoot production instead of a fixed
  calendar. NCSU lists stems, flower buds and flowers as edible. Oregon State's
  tight-bud harvest cue is identified as a practical cue from broccoli guidance,
  not a Broccolini-specific threshold. The UMN trial is evidence of variety
  differences, not a home-garden schedule.
- **Problems:** premature flowering is described only as a relative heat risk;
  flea beetles are reported only as the observation from one UMN trial, not as a
  universal diagnosis or resistance claim.
- **Use:** OSU describes a broccoli-like, sweeter / less pungent or
  asparagus-like flavor. No storage life or preservation process is claimed.

## Deliberately unresolved

- Named seed line/cultivar and cultivar-specific mature dimensions.
- Universal days-to-harvest, yield, number of repeat cuts, or heat threshold.
- Hydroponic suitability, pod count/spacing, indoor grow-light requirement.
- A Broccolini-specific storage duration or preservation procedure.
- Seed Profile germination range remains pending in its existing record and was
  not changed by this Plant Profile review.

No Garden adaptation was added. Existing Seed Profile, compatibility record,
private data, runtime code, publisher logic and schema were not changed for this
review.
