# Cardoon established-plant enrichment — 2026-10-18

`cardoon-cynara-cardunculus` remains the cardoon horticultural form of accepted
*Cynara cardunculus*, not a globe-artichoke cultivar. NC State's existing
species/crop-form reference supported an additional bilingual maintenance
section: trim older leaves; in its temperate outdoor context the plant can die
back and regrow from a deep taproot, with heavy winter mulch recommended; it
may become dormant in hot summers. These observations are explicitly not
presented as indoor-overwintering guarantees.

The Spanish growth, cultivation, and problem text was brought into meaningful
parity with the existing English claims, including the source's regional
3–6 ft height / 2–3 ft spread, full sun and rich moist but well-drained soil,
wind risk, reported problems, and the need to check local invasive status.
No new source, cultivar claim, hydroponic compatibility, or seed protocol was
added. Harvest Use and Seed Profile boundaries remain unchanged.

Source: NC State Extension, [European Cardoon (*Cynara cardunculus*)](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cynara-cardunculus/common-name/european-cardoon/).
