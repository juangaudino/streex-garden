# Carrot Harvest Use Cohort — 2026-10-17

## Scope

Added bilingual Harvest Use records for three existing identities:

- Nantes Carrot (`carrot-nantes`)
- Danvers Carrot (`danvers-carrot`)
- Imperator Carrot (`imperator-carrot`)

The Plant Profiles remain partial pending full editorial and auxiliary-layer review. The cohort adds post-harvest use; it is not a completion sign-off.

## Evidence and scope decisions

- USU Extension's nutrition guide supports fresh use, salads, soups, stir-fries, several cooking methods, juice, washing, and household storage advice for carrots generally.
- UMN Extension supports harvesting roots at a usable size, loosening soil before pulling, and removing/washing roots. Its guidance is Minnesota home-garden context.
- Johnny's describes Nantes as medium-length/cylindrical and Imperator as long/slender; these are type-level descriptors, not guarantees for every cultivar.
- USU lists Danvers Half Long among carrot variety options. Garden's `danvers-carrot` remains type-level and is not silently reidentified as that named selection.
- No cultivar/type-specific taste, recipe advantage, exact root dimension, storage duration guarantee, or indoor/hydroponic use was inferred. Crop-level culinary methods are intentionally similar because the consulted evidence does not distinguish them by type.
- Tops are removed for storage; this cohort does not assert foliage as an edible product. Canning safety steps were not reproduced as a recipe.
