## Editorial decisions

- **Stalk celery — *Apium graveolens*, stalk-celery horticultural type:** the
  established-plant profile now explains shallow-root/even-moisture care,
  temperature-related development context, selective outer-petiole versus
  whole-crown harvest cues, and recognizable blackheart, powdery-mildew, and
  aphid signs. Advice is crop-level and regional where applicable. Seed-starting
  guidance was removed from Plant Profile because Seed Profile owns that stage.
  Hydroponic fit, mature dimensions, and pod spacing remain unknown.
- **Chinese Light Green Celery — *Apium graveolens*, packet type unresolved:**
  the profile now focuses on established-crop conditions, harvest, quality
  problems, and appropriately scoped hydroponic context. The commercial product
  sources disagree between var. *secalinum* and var. *dulce*; the record remains
  pending rather than choosing a varietal identity. General celery evidence is
  not presented as product-specific proof. Seed germination remains in Seed
  Profile.
- **Florence fennel — *Foeniculum vulgare* var. *azoricum*:** added adult-plant
  cultivation context for the bulbing type, including full sun, fertile and
  moisture-retentive well-drained soil, moisture/bolting/splitting risks, and a
  bulb harvest cue plus cutting technique. The distinction from common/herb
  fennel is preserved. Germination was removed from Plant Profile because Seed
  Profile owns establishment. Hydroponic root/bulb geometry remains explicitly
  unvalidated, so this profile remains partial.
