# Chinese Light Green Celery — product-scope reconciliation (2026-10-19)

## Decision

Publish this identity at the accepted species and horticultural-type level:

- Accepted species: *Apium graveolens*.
- Represented crop type: Chinese/leaf celery.
- Catalog label: Chinese Light Green, a supplier-listed selection; formal
  cultivar status and exact varietas are not asserted.

This is sufficient to identify and usefully describe the represented plant
without choosing a disputed infraspecific label. POWO treats *A. graveolens*
var. *secalinum* as a synonym of *A. graveolens*. USDA GRIN records Chinese
celery as close or identical to var. *secalinum* and associates it with the
leaf-celery group. University of Hawaiʻi CTAHR separately describes Chinese
celery as an Asiatic type with fine, hollow leafy stalks used as a vegetable.
The supplier's Light Green listing supplies product-level morphology; it does
not establish a formal cultivar or exact taxonomic variety.

Commercial use of var. *dulce* for some Chinese celery products remains a label
conflict, but the catalog does not publish that label for this identity. It is
not a reason to leave the accepted species and horticultural type unresolved.

## Content boundary

The Plant Profile describes established celery. Seed-stage guidance remains in
Seed Profile. Crop-level soil care is not promoted to a compact hydroponic
prescription. The OSU EC/pH entry is shown only as a celery-crop reference; it
does not establish pod count or system performance. Supplier-reported
50–60-day timing remains explicitly approximate and product-scoped.

No packet quantity, purchase date, ownership, or private Garden context is
published.

## Evidence

- Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, POWO — accepted *Apium graveolens* and synonymy
  of var. *secalinum*.
- USDA ARS GRIN-Global — Chinese celery/leaf celery taxonomic and crop-group
  notes.
- University of Hawaiʻi CTAHR, “Chinese Celery,” publication 93-1-7 — Chinese
  celery type, morphology, garden context, harvest and use.
- FreeHeirloomSeeds CE1 listing — supplier-scoped Light Green product
  description and maturity estimate only.
