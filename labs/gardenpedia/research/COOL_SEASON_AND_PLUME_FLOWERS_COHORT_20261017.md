# Pansy and Plumosa celosia Plant Profile cohort — 2026-10-17

Two canonical public identities were individually reviewed and enriched in EN
and ES. Both remain `partial` in the editorial inventory: their outdoor
ornamental guidance is now useful and sourced, but indoor/hydroponic
compatibility remains unknown rather than inferred.

## `viola-pansy` — garden pansy

- Identity is restricted to the garden pansy hybrid *Viola × wittrockiana*;
  no color series or cultivar is assigned.
- Added rounded/mounded growth context, ranges reported by Iowa State and Texas
  Tech, outdoor full-to-part-sun and moist-soil guidance, deadheading, cool-season
  flowering/heat limitation, and bounded problems (heat, root rot, anthracnose,
  slugs).
- Texas Tech lists pansy flowers as edible, but neither reviewed source gives a
  preparation method or cultivar-specific food quality; the profile records that
  limit and does not invent a recipe or medicinal use.
- The seed profile exists and was not modified. Seed starting details were not
  copied into the established-plant profile.
- Sources: Iowa State University Extension,
  https://yardandgarden.extension.iastate.edu/how-to/growing-pansies-violas ;
  Texas Tech University Plant Resources,
  https://www.depts.ttu.edu/plantresources/Pages/directories/landscape-info-sheets/herbaceous-info/Viola_xwittrockiana.php .

## `celosia-plumosa` — Plumosa celosia

- Kew POWO accepts *Celosia argentea* and treats *C. argentea* var. *plumosa* as
  a synonym. The record remains the accepted species plus a horticultural
  Plumosa type, not a named cultivar.
- Mississippi State Extension's page title uses var. *plumosa*, while one
  sentence in its taxonomy paragraph inconsistently labels the ornamental group
  as var. *cristata* before listing Plumosa. We use POWO for accepted-name
  resolution and the Extension article only for its clearly identified Plumosa
  cut-flower husbandry; the nomenclature ambiguity is not propagated as a new
  canonical taxon.
- Added plume growth/production ranges with context, photoperiod effects on
  flowering and stems, the distinction that pinching is not required for
  Plumosa, fully-developed cut-flower harvest and drying, and aphid/thrips/
  powdery-mildew monitoring cues.
- The source's seed-stage light statement was explicitly excluded from mature
  light requirements. Mature light, home hydroponic suitability, and a single
  household size remain unknown. Field spacing was not converted to pod spacing.
- Sources: Kew POWO,
  https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:3233583-4 ;
  Mississippi State University Extension,
  https://extension.msstate.edu/publications/celosia-argentea-var-plumosa-for-the-farmer-florist .
- The seed profile exists and was not modified.

The historical `bachelor-button` and `snapdragon` entries were not edited:
catalog reconciliation shows the public identities are `cornflower-centaurea-cyanus`
and `snapdragon-antirrhinum-majus`; the latter is already marked complete. This
avoids enriching a noncanonical alias or creating duplicate public content.

## Disposition

No Plant or Seed schema changes were made. No new Harvest Use record was added:
the pansy source establishes only edible status, without a supported
preparation/use method, and Celosia is represented as an ornamental cut flower.
Both inventory records remain partial pending editorial consideration of their
unknown Garden-system fit.
