# Corn salad whole-record review — 2026-10-18

## Disposition

`corn-salad-valeriana-locusta` passes the Plant Profile gate for the species
identity *Valeriana locusta*, with horticultural synonym *Valerianella
locusta*. The record is not a named cultivar. Its outdoor crop guidance,
adult maintenance, repeated/whole-rosette harvest, use, warning signs, source
scope and EN/ES overlay were reviewed together.

## Corrections and enrichment

- Kew POWO accepts *Valeriana locusta*, lists *Valerianella locusta* as a
  synonym, and classifies the species as annual or biennial. USU's gardening
  guide uses *Valerianella locusta*; the profile preserves both usages instead
  of silently rewriting the source's name.
- USU documents full outdoor sun, fertile well-drained soil, steady moisture,
  best quality around 75°F or below, heat-related quality loss/flowering,
  weed competition, leaf harvest, and whole-small-plant harvest. Soil spacing
  and climate guidance are not converted to indoor pods or grow-light values.
- USU reports refrigeration for five to seven days and washing before use; a
  bilingual Harvest Use method now presents that as source guidance, not a
  universal household shelf-life guarantee.
- A previous English/Spanish claim about aphids and curled leaves was removed:
  the linked USU guide does not list aphids. Remaining problem examples are
  the guide's flea beetles, slugs, leaf miners and Alternaria leaf spot, with
  the source's recognizable signs retained.

## Explicit limits

Hydroponic suitability, mature dimensions and indoor grow-light need remain
unknown. The accepted species' annual/biennial classification is not treated as
a guaranteed one-season lifespan in every garden. No Seed Profile or private
seed inventory was changed.
