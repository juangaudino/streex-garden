# Cosmos established-plant completion cohort — 2026-10-18

Reviewed full EN/ES Plant Profiles, compatibility evidence, scope, source
references and existing Seed Profile links for three *Cosmos bipinnatus* records.
No Seed Profile content was changed.

## Dispositions

- **Sensation Cosmos — complete.** The public identity is the *C. bipinnatus*
  ‘Sensation’ series, not a specific cultivar. NC State directly lists its large
  pink/red/white flowers; its species guidance supports outdoor care, deadheading,
  conditional staking, cut-flower use, and possible disease/pest signals. Added a
  bilingual flowering section. Compatibility habit/light evidence now cites the
  Extension source that actually supports it, not a seed-starting guide. Exact
  series size and hydroponic fit remain unknown.
- **Seashell Cosmos — complete.** NC State directly identifies Seashells Mix,
  its rolled tubular petals, red/pink/white color range, and approximate 3 ft
  height. The reported mix height is now represented in Compatibility Profile V1
  at identity scope, rounded to 91 cm; species-level outdoor care, light,
  management and problems remain properly scoped. It is documented for cut
  flowers, but no exact vase life or mix-specific cutting stage is claimed.
- **Dwarf Cosmos — remains partial.** Species-level care, adult management,
  harvesting, problems and EN/ES content are coherent, but this catalog record
  is not linked to a named compact cultivar or a defined dwarf class. The prior
  “mounded” compatibility label lacked evidence for that controlled value and
  was changed to unknown. A height mistakenly borrowed from NC State's separate
  Seashells Mix listing was also removed; Dwarf Cosmos mature height remains
  unknown. No cultivar dimensions or habit were inferred.
