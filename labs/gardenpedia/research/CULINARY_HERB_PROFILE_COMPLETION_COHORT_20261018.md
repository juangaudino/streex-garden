# Culinary herb Plant Profile completion cohort — 2026-10-18

Four existing herb profiles passed individual review against the Plant Profile
completion rubric. The pass checked established-plant guidance, useful harvest
or use, EN/ES parity, source resolution/scope, and whether known unknowns affect
the core gardening guidance.

- **Curry Leaf Tree** — accepted *Bergera koenigii* with *Murraya koenigii* as
  synonym; UC Master Gardener climate, frost, light and moisture advice remains
  regional. Fresh/frozen leaf use is covered; no universal dimensions or
  hydroponic fit is claimed.
- **German Chamomile** — resolved as *Matricaria chamomilla*, distinct from
  other plants called chamomile; established soil/light, deadheading versus
  seed set, flower harvest near full bloom and aphid context are covered.
- **Mexican Tarragon** — *Tagetes lucida* remains distinct from French tarragon.
  UF/IFAS Florida freeze and flowering context is not generalized into a
  universal hardiness calendar; conservative harvest and culinary use are
  represented.
- **Tulsi** — *Ocimum tenuiflorum* remains distinct from *O. basilicum*.
  Established growth, cultivation, harvest/use and self-seeding context are
  present; no medicinal efficacy, dosing or hydroponic suitability is asserted.

Unresolved hydroponic fit or exact mature dimensions were left unknown because
they are not necessary to answer the profiles' documented general cultivation
needs. No new source or canonical fact was added in this review. Optional visual,
neighbor and evidence-companion records were not used as completion quotas.
