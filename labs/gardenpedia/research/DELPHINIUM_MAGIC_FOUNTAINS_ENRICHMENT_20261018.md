# Magic Fountains delphinium Plant Profile enrichment — 2026-10-18

Expanded the existing `delphinium-magic-fountains-dwarf` public enrichment
using two Royal Horticultural Society references. No Seed Profile, private
instance data, or runtime behavior was changed.

- RHS identifies the Magic Fountain perennial series as a smaller delphinium
  type around 1 m tall. This is series-level evidence, not a guaranteed size
  for every plant in the mixed packet.
- RHS's separate *Delphinium elatum* profile gives species-level culture,
  support, post-flowering pruning, pests/diseases and harmful-if-eaten guidance.
  Tall-type support advice is not made mandatory for this dwarf mix.
- Exact packet selection/performance, container behavior and indoor/hydroponic
  fit remain unknown. They do not prevent the general ornamental Plant Profile
  from answering identity, established care, flowering, safe ornamental use and
  recognizable problems; the profile passed its individual completion gate on
  2026-10-18.
