# Edible Crop Harvest Use — Three-Profile Review (2026-10-18)

This small editorial pass adds practical, bilingual post-harvest guidance to
three existing identities whose Plant Profiles already describe established
crop care, but whose Harvest Use layer was absent. It adds no plant identity,
Seed Profile, or agronomic compatibility claim.

## Decisions and evidence scope

- **Honey Bear F1 acorn squash:** Illinois Extension's winter-squash guidance
  explicitly includes acorn squash for cooking and freezing. The record uses
  crop/type-level preparation and preservation guidance only; it does not
  assign a Honey Bear-specific flavor, recipe, or shelf-life claim.
- **Patio Tomato:** USU's tomato guide supports fully colored, still-firm
  harvest, end-of-season handling, and the need to treat its regional storage
  figures as contextual. NCHFP supports a tested-process warning for
  shelf-stable tomato preservation. The catalog record remains a horticultural
  type rather than a named cultivar, and no processing recipe is reproduced.
- **Stalk Celery:** USU Extension supports selective removal of outer stalks
  and whole-plant maturity cues. NCHFP's celery freezing instructions support
  blanching and cooked-use-only handling after freezing. Neither source is
  represented as cultivar-specific.

## Validation boundary

The three records are EN/ES, every method references existing registered or
Harvest Use-local source metadata, and their IDs match the 501-row public
inventory. The merged Harvest Use layer now contains 354 unique identity
records. This is not a claim that all remaining catalog identities need an
edible-use card.
