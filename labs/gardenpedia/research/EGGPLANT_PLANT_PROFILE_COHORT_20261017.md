## Evidence and scope

- University of Minnesota Extension, [Growing eggplant in home gardens](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-eggplant): warm-season crop; install support at planting; maintain even soil moisture and water at the base; harvest firm, glossy fruit with shears; continued harvest can support continued flowering/fruit set; describes flea-beetle holes, spider-mite discoloration, Verticillium wilt and other crop-level signs. Minnesota weather thresholds and soil/rainfall amounts were not converted into universal or indoor recommendations.
- Kew POWO, [Solanum melongena](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/820053-1): species identity only; not cultivar evidence.
- Johnny's, [Hansel F1](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/vegetables/eggplant/mini-eggplants/hansel-f1-eggplant-seed-240.51.html): named hybrid, compact plant/fruit traits and product maturity; shared crop instructions are not treated as Hansel-only trial results. Its description names grilling and thin pizza slices as culinary uses.
- Johnny's, [Fairy Tale F1](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/vegetables/eggplant/mini-eggplants/fairy-tale-f1-eggplant-seed-2260.50.html): named hybrid, compact dimensions, container product feature and fruit traits. These claims remain product-scoped.
- Johnny's, [Patio Baby F1](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/vegetables/eggplant/mini-eggplants/patio-baby-f1-eggplant-seed-3747.54.html): named hybrid, container suitability, compact dimensions and small-fruit product claims. Shared growing text is used only at crop scope.
- Cornell Cooperative Extension, [Black Beauty variety entry](https://vegvariety.cce.cornell.edu/main/detail.php?filterBy=0&variety_id=236): participatory variety reports about fruit and plant habit, not a controlled cultivar trial or guaranteed seed-line performance.
- Iowa State University Extension, [Eggplant](https://spendsmart.extension.iastate.edu/produce-item/eggplant/): household preparation examples, edible skin and short-term food handling. Its refrigeration guidance is shown alongside UMN's warning that prolonged refrigeration can pit eggplant skin; no universal storage duration is promised.

## Editorial decisions

- `Black Beauty` remains a named type without a represented seed producer or
  line. Cornell's reported dimensions are explicitly observational. The UMN
  garden guidance remains crop/species scoped.
- `Hansel F1` and `Fairy Tale F1` retain their distinct fruit traits and
  product-level dimensions. Container suitability is asserted only for Fairy
  Tale because Johnny's lists it; no such inference is made for Hansel.
- `Patio Baby F1` keeps Johnny's container claim at product scope; compact size
  is not treated as proof of support-free growth or hydroponic compatibility.
- The new care sections address moisture and base watering without importing
  Minnesota rainfall into a universal schedule. Support, pest and disease
  information is labeled crop-level; no resistance or cultivar-specific
  diagnosis is claimed.
- Harvest Use distinguishes Hansel's vendor-named grilling/pizza uses from
  general eggplant preparation. For Fairy Tale and Black Beauty, no special
  recipe advantage is asserted. No validated companion relation or
  instructional visual source was found for this cohort, so neither was
  fabricated.
- Hydroponic suitability remains unknown for all four. No indoor-light,
  pod-spacing or nutrient-engine mapping was added.
