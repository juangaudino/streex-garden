# English Ivy — indoor-culture completion review (2026-10-18)

Identity reviewed: `english-ivy-hedera-helix`, accepted *Hedera helix*,
cultivar unspecified.

## Evidence and scope

- University of Missouri Extension, [Caring for Houseplants](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g6510): its cultural-preference table names English ivy and lists bright ambient light, evenly moist conditions, and approximately 50°F night temperature. The same document gives general houseplant guidance on drainage, porous potting medium, thorough watering and discarding excess water; those substrate/drainage points are not Hedera-specific.
- University of Missouri Extension, [Lighting Indoor Houseplants](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g6515): ivy varieties are described as doing best in medium-to-bright light, needing consistent moisture to reduce leaf drop, and growing better somewhat cool indoors. These are ambient houseplant conditions, not a quantified grow-light recommendation.
- Existing Kew and NC State Extension sources remain responsible for species identity, juvenile/adult growth, outdoor landscape behavior, pruning/containment and toxicity. NC State's invasive warning remains North Carolina-scoped.
