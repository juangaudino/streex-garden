# Escarole established-plant review — 2026-10-18

## Disposition

`escarole` passes the Plant Profile editorial gate at the broad-leaved escarole
horticultural-type level within *Cichorium endivia*. No named cultivar is
represented. The inventory was updated from partial to complete after checking
the existing record, its Harvest Use entry, bilingual section parity, and the
scope of every claim.

## Useful established-plant content

- The UMN Extension guide treats escarole as a broad, smooth-leaved form of
  endive and groups it with cool-season chicories.
- It describes the shallow horizontal roots as the main water/nutrient uptake
  zone, well-drained but moisture-retentive garden soil, and soil pH 6.0–6.8 for
  lettuce and chicories. These remain Minnesota soil-garden guidance, not
  hydroponic solution values.
- Harvest can be individual usable leaves, leaving inner leaves for continued
  growth, or a whole-plant cut at/just above soil level. The guide says cooked
  escarole is commonly used in recipes; its pre-harvest watering note concerns
  crispness, not a guaranteed yield or maturity date.
- A new maintenance section describes the source's endive blanching technique
  as an optional type-level choice: tying outer leaves around the heart,
  approximately two weeks in the cited guidance. It is not presented as
  necessary or cultivar-specific.
- Problem guidance distinguishes bolting/strong bitterness and tipburn (brown
  leaf edges after watering resumes from a dry spell); crop-group pest signs
  are retained at the UMN scope.

## Evidence and limits

- University of Minnesota Extension: identity/type distinction and regional

  (`umn-lettuce-endive-radicchio-wave29`).
- The existing Harvest Use record remains separate and covers edible leaves,
  cooked use, and its own source-linked methods; no duplicate record was added.
- The source is regional and often speaks at chicory/endive or leafy-crop
  scope. No cultivar-specific maturity, disease resistance, dimensions,
  indoor grow-light tier, hydroponic suitability, or pod spacing was inferred.
  Unknown compatibility values remain explicit.
