# Flax (*Linum usitatissimum*) — established Plant Profile review

Individually reviewed the species-level record and translated the same claim
set into EN and ES. The profile now distinguishes fiber flax from seed/oil
flax, whose stature, branching, production density, and harvest endpoint
differ. It adds field-scope height context, outdoor full-sun and soil guidance,
flower development, the brown-boll seed harvest cue, the earlier fiber harvest
stage, post-harvest fiber processing, seed uses, and bounded pest/safety
caveats.

Regional commercial field recommendations are labeled as such. Rates and
stand densities are not converted to home or hydroponic spacing. The 90%
brown-boll cue and North Dakota maturity values are not turned into a
universal calendar. Fiber retting is described as a required processing step,
not as a household how-to. NCSU's warning about large-quantity ingestion and
contact irritation is retained alongside Extension sources describing food
uses; this profile does not invent a safe dose, food-processing procedure, or
household storage duration.

The profile confirms seed as a propagation mode but deliberately does not
duplicate a seed-to-seedling protocol. No cultivar/production purpose, garden
dimensions, indoor grow-light tier, hydroponic fit, or pod spacing is assigned.
No Seed Profile or Harvest Use auxiliary record was created; the Plant Profile
itself covers the crop's harvest/use distinction at the level supported by the
evidence. No private data or runtime/schema changes.

## Evidence

- Kew, [*Linum usitatissimum* — Plants of the World Online](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:544772-1) — accepted species identity.
- NC State Extension, [Flax — *Linum usitatissimum*](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/linum-usitatissimum/common-name/flax/) — species profile, outdoor culture, flowering, propagation mode, occasional pests, and toxicity/contact warning; perennial-flax pruning text was not applied.
- University of Wisconsin–Madison Extension, [Flax](https://cropsandsoils.extension.wisc.edu/flax/) — Wisconsin fiber/seed production distinctions, regional field management, harvest windows, and processing.
- North Dakota State University Extension, [Flax Production in North Dakota (A1038)](https://www.library.nd.gov/statedocs/NDSUExtensionService/a1038flaxproductionnd20161013.pdf) — field growth, type differences, flowering, 90%-brown-boll seed maturity cue, and production pest/disease context.

Disposition: complete under the current Plant Profile rubric. The identity is
publishable at species level; type-specific and household-scale unknowns remain
explicit rather than blocking useful general guidance.
