# Garden Angelica root-harvest conflict — 2026-10-18

Identity: `garden-angelica-angelica-archangelica` — *Angelica archangelica*.

## Evidence checked

- [University of Missouri Extension: Growing Herbs at Home (G6470)](https://extension.missouri.edu/publications/g6470) says in its general root section that Angelica roots are dug in late fall or early spring; its species-specific Angelica entry says roots and leaves are collected in late summer of the second year. These recommendations conflict within the same publication.
- [UNH Extension: How to grow angelica](https://extension.unh.edu/resource/how-grow-angelica-angelica-archangelica) says leaves and stalks are best used young and tender and seeds collected in late summer or early fall; it lists roots among edible portions but gives no root timing or technique.
- [NC State Extension: Wild Celery / *Angelica archangelica*](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/angelica-archangelica/common-name/wild-celery/) supports identity, culinary use of roots/leaves/seeds/young stems, and cautions about toxic lookalikes and photosensitivity/contact dermatitis; it does not resolve root timing.
- [RHS: How to grow angelica](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/angelica/how-to-grow-angelica) adds useful established-plant guidance: deep taproot and poor container suitability, dislike of root disturbance, cutting back after flowering, and the possibility that *A. archangelica* persists another year if flowers are removed before seed set. It does not specify root harvest timing.
- [OMAFRA: Angelica — Specialty Cropportunities](https://www.omafra.gov.on.ca/cropop/en/herbs/culinary/angel.html) is an *A. archangelica*-specific Ontario crop profile. It gives a single root harvest in early autumn after year two and describes commercial harvest using a modified potato harvester. This adds a regional production context, not a household root-harvest procedure.
- Do not use the separate OMAFRA medicinal-herbs PDF's second-year root timing as evidence for *A. archangelica*: the cited passage is about *Angelica sinensis* (dang gui), a different species.

## Editorial decision

Keep leaf/stalk and ripe-seed timing source-backed. Present OMAFRA's Ontario commercial root schedule separately from Missouri's internally conflicting dates; do not generalize commercial machinery or timing into a household protocol. The public profile now gives the user supported outdoor site, deep-root/container and post-flowering management guidance, plus the distinct harvest contexts. Root use remains qualified; no universal household root-harvest technique or medicinal advice is inferred. Individual EN/ES/source-scope review passed the Plant Profile gate.
