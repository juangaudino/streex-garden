# Gardyn ornamental Plant Profile review cohort — 2026-10-18

## Scope and dispositions

Reviewed Black Viola (*Viola cornuta*), Blush Hibiscus
(*Muenchhusia moscheutos*, syn. *Hibiscus moscheutos*) and Pink Petunia
(*Petunia × atkinsiana*, syn. *P. × hybrida*). In all cases Gardyn's product
label is not treated as a named cultivar or as universal evidence for other
growing systems.

- **Black Viola — remains partial.** Added NC State species-level outdoor
  moisture/light context, heat-related flowering behavior, deadheading/cutback,
  broad dimensions and pest cues. The species source lists edible leaves and
  flowers, but the dark-flowered Gardyn product's food-use handling is not
  established; Garden adaptation therefore keeps it ornamental-only. The source
  system's pod instructions are not generalized to mature hydroponic performance.
- **Blush Hibiscus — now complete after a product-source review on 2026-10-18.**
  Gardyn's current *Hibiscus (Blush & Red)* guide supplies established-system
  Medium Light Zone, approximate 2–3 ft product size, root-check/root-trim
  instructions, topping and retained-height guidance, support, successive
  flowering waves, a normal-discoloration caveat, and cut-flower harvest. These
  claims are now explicitly limited to the Gardyn product; they are not mapped
  to generic indoor-light tiers or other hydroponic systems. NC State's broad
  outdoor species dimensions, soil/light context, pests and flowering remain
  separately scoped. Its fall-cleanup page conflicts (cut nearly to ground,
  but also leave 12–24-inch stems for nesting bees), so no universal fall rule
  was selected. Gardyn's edible-part claim is reported only as product guidance;
  independent food-safety eligibility and treatment history remain unverified,
  and no broad culinary Harvest Use record was added. EN/ES and section-source
  references were corrected. Compatibility values and nutrient mappings were
  not changed; non-Gardyn hydroponic performance remains unknown.
- **Pink Petunia — now complete after a product-source review on 2026-10-18.**
  Gardyn's current product guide supplies its Medium Light Zone and temperature
  context, approximate size and cascading/fragrant pink-flower description,
  monthly root checks, support, deadheading, pruning, stickiness note, and an
  explicit non-edible warning. These claims remain specific to the Gardyn
  product and are not translated into a generalized light tier or other-system
  compatibility. UMN/Clemson outdoor Petunia guidance remains separate. The
  commercial record does not identify a cultivar or formal horticultural group;
  those unknowns do not block guidance for the represented Gardyn product. EN/ES
  and source references were checked; compatibilityProfile was not changed.

## Evidence and validation notes

- NC State's *V. cornuta* guidance is species-level and is not assigned as a
  dark-flowered cultivar claim. Its “full sun to part shade” wording is not
  collapsed into a partial-sun category; only full sun is represented in the
  existing compatibility contract, with the shade limitation recorded.
- NC State's *M. moscheutos* source supports species-level outdoor claims only;
  it does not validate the unnamed Gardyn selection's performance outside the
  manufacturer-specific guide.
- UMN's petunia rules remain horticultural-group-specific. No product group,
  cultivar dimensions, or grow-light setting was inferred.
- Source registry was extended for the NC State hibiscus profile and the UMN
  scope note was clarified for its cut-flower guidance. The existing NC State
  Viola source record was reused rather than duplicated.
- EN/ES sections were checked for parity. No Seed Profile, private user data,
  Supabase, UI, publisher code, or runtime behavior was changed in this cohort.
