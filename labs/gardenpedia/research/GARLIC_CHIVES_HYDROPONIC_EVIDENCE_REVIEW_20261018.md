# Garlic chives: hydroponic evidence review — 2026-10-18

## Decision

Enriched the public Plant Profile for `garlic-chives` with one species-level,
primary-study section. Kept `compatibilityProfile` and nutrient-engine mappings
unchanged: the study establishes that *Allium tuberosum* has been experimentally
cultivated hydroponically, but does not establish compatibility with each home
countertop appliance or Garden system.

## Evidence and scope

Ahn and Ikeda's 2004 paper in *Horticultural Research (Japan)* tested seedlings
and cut plants of *Allium tuberosum* at solution pH 4.5, 5.5, 6.5 and 7.5 and
at 1/4, 1/2, 1 and 2 times a standard nutrient concentration. The paper reports
reduced seedling growth at the highest concentration and reduced regrowth at pH
4.5 and at the highest concentration; the authors estimated pH 5–7 and 1/4 to
1 standard concentration for their hydroponic production context. These results
are presented as study-specific evidence, not a general consumer recipe, an EC
conversion, a formula-specific instruction, or a calculator/Nutrient Engine
mapping.

The profile's hydroponic suitability for home systems remains unknown. No
private seed ownership or packet data was changed.
