## Editorial decisions

- **Cast-Iron Plant — *Aspidistra elatior*:** NC State Extension supports the
  shade/soil context, rhizomatous clumping habit, seasonal outdoor-region
  watering guidance, rare ground-level flowering, and its report of no serious
  pest/disease problems. Cultivar dimensions shown on the page are not assigned
  to this species-level profile. Indoor light tiers and hydroponic suitability
  remain unknown.
- **Arrowhead Plant — *Syngonium podophyllum*:** the profile clarifies the
  juvenile-to-adult leaf/habit change, pruning and repotting management, likely
  pests/disease signals, and the species' calcium-oxalate hazard. It does not
  transfer named cultivar traits or infer controlled-system compatibility.
- **Dwarf Umbrella Tree — *Heptapleurum arboricola*:** the profile distinguishes
  container size from outdoor tropical dimensions; gives the source's filtered
  natural-light, drying/soaking, and temperature context; and notes rare indoor
  flowering, moisture-related leaf drop, pests, and the species' pet/sap safety
  risks. Cultivar-specific claims and hydroponic fit remain unknown. The
  extension page lists seed propagation but supplies no seed-start protocol, so
  no Seed Profile was fabricated from that listing alone.
