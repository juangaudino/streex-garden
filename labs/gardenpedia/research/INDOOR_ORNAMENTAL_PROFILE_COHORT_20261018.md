## Evidence and editorial decisions

### African violet — *Streptocarpus ionanthus*

NC State Extension's species page supports filtered indoor light, a fertile
fast-draining medium, crown placement, bottom watering, surface-dry timing,
humidity and temperature context, rosette morphology, broad cultivar-size/form
variation, and recognizable water-related, cold, pest and disease concerns.
The public identity remains species-level and no cultivar size or hydroponic
recipe is inferred. Leaf/stem cutting information is retained as propagation;
the source does not provide a seed-start protocol.

### Parlor palm — *Chamaedorea elegans*

NC State Extension supports low-light tolerance alongside bright-indirect-light
preference, container mix and moisture guidance, slow solitary-stem growth,
indoor size context, repotting and seasonal feeding, spider mites/mealybugs,
and a caution that rare fruit pulp may irritate sensitive skin. Grouped nursery
plants are not treated as a multi-stem species identity. No controlled-system
size or hydroponic compatibility is inferred.

### Rainbow Mix coleus — *Coleus scutellarioides*

The supplier listing supports the seed-product blend identity; Kew supports the
accepted species name; University of Minnesota Extension supplies crop-level
adult care. Its broad 6–36 inch height range and variable width are explicitly
kept as species/crop context, not guaranteed blend dimensions. The profile
records cold sensitivity, cultivar-dependent sun selection, pinch/flower
management and generally low pest/disease pressure. It does not assign the mix
an individual light preference, color pattern, mature size, indoor light tier,
or hydroponic compatibility.

## Disposition

All three records passed the Plant Profile editorial gate for their represented
ornamental identities. Harvest Use is not applicable as edible-crop guidance.
Unknown Garden-system compatibility remains explicit. Existing taxonomy,
public identity IDs, Seed Profiles and canonical contracts were not changed.
