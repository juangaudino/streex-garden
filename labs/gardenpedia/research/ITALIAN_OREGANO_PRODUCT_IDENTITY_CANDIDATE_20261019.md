# Italian Oregano — product identity candidate (2026-10-19)

## Finding

A public listing for the HOME GROWN 10-herb kit (model HG10H) names its Italian
Oregano component as *Origanum × majoricum*. This is a useful product-specific
candidate, but it does not by itself establish that every packet sold as
“Italian Oregano” by the brand belongs to this kit or that the catalog identity
represents this exact selection.

Other commercial seed listings use “Italian Oregano” for *Origanum vulgare*
forms, while extension and botanical references differ in how they use the
common name. The label “Italian Oregano” is therefore not sufficient to choose
between *O. × majoricum* and *O. vulgare*.

## Disposition

Keep `italian-oregano` pending. Do not change its accepted scientific identity,
Seed Profile, or species-scoped agronomy based on the HG10H listing alone. A
matching packet image, UPC/model identifier, or explicit confirmation that the
catalog record represents the HG10H kit component would be needed before
adopting this product-level identity. If confirmed, retain *O. × majoricum* as
the specific product identity and keep claims from broader oregano sources at
their actual scope.
