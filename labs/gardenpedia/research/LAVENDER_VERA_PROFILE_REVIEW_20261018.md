# Lavender Vera — profile review (2026-10-18)

## Evidence and scope

Utah State University Extension identifies English lavender as *Lavandula angustifolia* and explicitly lists Vera among English-lavender varieties. Its advice on soil, habit, pruning, and flowering is general English-lavender guidance, not a Vera-specific performance trial. Johnny's harvest advice describes cutting spikes just before flowers open; USU distinguishes first-year branch clipping from harvest of established flowering spikes.
