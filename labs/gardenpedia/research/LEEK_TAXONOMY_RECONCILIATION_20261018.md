# Leek taxonomy and profile cohort — 2026-10-18

## Decision

For `leek-american-flag`, `king-richard-leek`, and
`giant-musselburgh-leek`, Gardenpedia's accepted scientific-name field now uses
*Allium porrum*, following Kew POWO. The formal synonym *Allium ampeloprasum*
var. *porrum* is retained. The horticultural Leek/Porrum Group treatment and
alternative taxonomic usage of *Allium ampeloprasum* remain documented as
nomenclatural context rather than being erased or presented as an unresolved
cultivar identity. This reconciliation does not merge or rename the three
Gardenpedia identities.

Kew POWO accepts *Allium porrum*, lists *A. ampeloprasum* var. *porrum* among
its homotypic synonyms, and notes authorities that use an alternative taxonomy
citing *A. ampeloprasum*. USDA ARS GRIN records the varietal name as a synonym
and cites horticultural Leek/Porrum Group treatments. UW–Madison Extension
uses both *A. porrum* and *A. ampeloprasum* var. *porrum* in its leek article
and separately lists the three named varieties.

## Content scope

UW–Madison's named-variety descriptions support only the claims attached to
each listed entry: American Flag's 130-day reference and 7–9-inch blanched
stems; King Richard's 75-day reference, early slender stems, and mini-leek use;
and Giant Musselburgh's 150-day reference and late large tender stalks. Dates
are source references, not universal schedules or Garden-system dimensions.
The same article supports crop-level full sun, well-drained soil, gradual
blanching, an optional midsummer leaf cut, harvest around one inch diameter,
and recognizable thrips/maggot signals. Soil spacing was not converted into
Garden pod spacing. No hydroponic suitability, indoor light tier, mature
height/spread, or cultivar-specific pruning response was inferred.

The existing Harvest Use records were retained and checked against the
identity-specific crop/variety distinction. Seed Profiles were not modified:
seed-stage guidance remains separate from established-plant content.
