## Evidence and scope

- [NCSU Extension Gardener Plant Toolbox: Lablab](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/lablab-purpureus/common-name/lablab/) supports accepted/common identity context, outdoor care, height range, edible young pods and mature-seed preparation cautions. Illinois Extension's [Hyacinth Bean](https://extension.illinois.edu/flowers/hyacinth-bean) supports vigorous ornamental growth, flowers and its separate height description. The 10–25 ft and up-to-30-ft source ranges remain visibly in conflict rather than being collapsed into a false precise size.
- [UMN Extension: Growing beans in home gardens](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-beans) supports home-garden maturity/use context for edamame, hyacinth bean, yardlong bean and common-bean snap stages. Minnesota timing and wildlife context remain regional.
- [UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions: Winged Bean](https://gardeningsolutions.ifas.ufl.edu/plants/edibles/vegetables/winged-bean/) supports the young-pod cue, regional food uses, support, moisture and short-day/day-neutral cultivar distinction. Root use is not promised for an unspecified selection.
- [UF/IFAS: Long Bean (HS1268)](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/HS1268) supports yardlong identity, climbing outdoor crop habit, Florida production context, repeated immature-pod harvest and cooked use. Its field calendar and spacing are not represented as universal home-garden settings.
- [UF/IFAS 2025–2026 Vegetable Production Handbook, Chapter 11](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/CV125) supports the crop-level warning that many edamame varieties are daylength-sensitive and that flowering classes need regional selection. It does not establish a named cultivar, home-system spacing or indoor-light tier.
- The existing Johnny's Provider product source continues to support only the named selection's product/pod/resistance description. General snap-bean harvest/use relies on UMN crop guidance; resistance codes are not immunity claims.

## Boundaries and residual questions

- Seed-stage soaking/scarification remains only in the corresponding Seed Profiles; Plant Profiles describe established-plant care.
- No hydroponic compatibility or Garden pod spacing was inferred for these field/outdoor legume sources.
- Edamame remains partial because indoor light and pruning are not established. Provider remains partial because no evidence-backed pruning guidance was identified; its other claims were not generalized from the product page.
- Harvest Use was added for Provider, Hyacinth Bean, Winged Bean and Yardlong Bean. Edamame already had a bilingual Harvest Use record.
- EN/ES section coverage, source references and taxonomy scope were checked for all five.

## Common-bean profile completion follow-up — 2026-10-18

Four additional common-bean identities passed a whole-record review: Blue Lake
Pole Bean, Blue Lake Bush Bean, Royal Burgundy Bush Bean, and Provider Bush
Bean. UMN Extension supports common-bean type distinctions, soil moisture,
snap-pod maturity, repeat harvest, and heat-related flower/pod-set problems;
NCSU supports species-level full sun. Johnny's and West Coast Seeds claims
remain scoped to their named products. Burpee's Blue Lake 274 listing is used
only to clarify that the broad Blue Lake bush type is not being asserted to be
that one selection. Empty, unsupported pruning placeholders were removed
instead of being treated as required crop content. No Seed Profile was changed.

The compatibility value for Blue Lake Pole's climbing habit remains unknown:
V1 has no climbing/vining enum, and mapping it to `trailing` would be inaccurate.
The Plant Profile itself clearly explains the climbing habit and trellis need.
All four profiles now pass the bilingual Plant Profile gate; dimensions and
hydroponic fit remain unknown where sources do not establish them.

Inventory after this follow-up: **321 complete, 148 partial, 32 pending** (501
canonical identities). The validator reads 506 raw Plant records, while the

## Adzuki home-garden follow-up — 2026-10-18

Adzuki Bean (*Vigna angularis*) passed an individual whole-record gate after
adding UF/IFAS's Florida home-garden guide (HS549/MV016). It describes a usually
erect 1–2 ft plant while noting reports of indeterminate growth, consistent
soil moisture, frost-sensitive timing, no-trellis care in its described context,
and both a young snap-pod stage and dry-seed harvest. These claims are scoped to
that regional home-garden guide and reconciled with Wisconsin's broader record
of erect and branching/vining cultivated forms. The profile and Harvest Use now
distinguish green tender-pod harvest from dry seed; EN/ES were updated together.
The source's Florida dates and row spacing were not generalized to containers or
hydroponics. Exact seed-selection habit, compact-system fit and hydroponic
suitability remain unknown, without blocking this useful species-level outdoor
profile. The separate Seed Profile and private seed inventory were unchanged.

Sources: [UF/IFAS Adzuki Bean](https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/MV016),
[University of Wisconsin–Madison Agronomy](https://corn.agronomy.wisc.edu/Crops/AdzukiBean.aspx),
and existing Oregon State Extension crop-production evidence.
