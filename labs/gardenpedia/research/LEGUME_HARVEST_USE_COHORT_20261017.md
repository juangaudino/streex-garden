# Legume Harvest Use cohort — 2026-10-17

## Scope and disposition

Added bilingual Harvest Use records for six existing seed-grown identities:
adzuki bean, chickpea, lentil, mung bean, pigeon pea, and scarlet runner bean.
No new identities or Seed Profiles were created. Adzuki and pigeon pea Plant
Profile harvest sections were updated with newly cited evidence. All six
inventory statuses remain pending: this work fills a useful content layer but
does not constitute a full identity-by-identity profile audit.

Butterfly pea was not included in this edible-legume cohort; its relevant
harvest is flower use and belongs in a flower-focused editorial review.

## Evidence and claim scope

- Oregon State University Extension, [Adzuki Bean: A Potential Rotational Crop for the Columbia Basin](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/pub/em-9332): adzuki-specific sweet-paste and other food uses; regional field maturity cues (senescing leaves, yellow pods, hard seeds that rattle). The Columbia Basin calendar and field dimensions were not generalized to home growing or to an unnamed cultivar.
- University of Alaska Fairbanks Cooperative Extension, [Cooking Dried Beans, Peas & Lentils](https://www.uaf.edu/ces/publications/database/food/beans-peas-and-lentils.php): named food-use/preparation material for chickpeas, lentils, mung beans, and adzuki sprouts, plus general dry-legume handling. No crop harvest date was derived from this food-preparation guide. Its seed-safety caveat is retained: garden seed is not automatically food-grade or suitable for sprouting.
- University of Maine Cooperative Extension, [Legumes: Dry Beans, Peas, and Lentils](https://extension.umaine.edu/publications/4183e/): chickpea and lentil culinary uses, preparation and household storage. Storage claims are not presented as guaranteed shelf life for every household.
- Colorado State University Extension, [Cooking Dry Beans](https://extension.colostate.edu/resource/cooking-dry-beans/): general dry-bean sorting, cooking and storage, with an explicit no-soak note for lentils. This is not species-specific crop production guidance.
- UW–Madison Extension, [Chickpea — Field Crops](https://corn.aae.wisc.edu/Crops/Chickpea.aspx): chickpea dry-pulse/green-vegetable distinction and commercial maturity/harvest context. Field machinery and moisture specifications were not converted into home-garden instructions.
- Texas A&M AgriLife Extension, [Pigeon Peas](https://aggie-horticulture.tamu.edu/vegetable/guides/specialty-vegetables/pigeon-peas/): species-level description and Texas field-crop context; most market harvest is reported at dry stage. Its approximate regional time-to-harvest was not assigned as a home-garden calendar, and green-shell timing remains unestablished.
- UW–Madison Extension, [Scarlet Runner Bean, Phaseolus coccineus](https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/scarlet-runner-bean-phaseolus-coccineus/): species-specific edible flowers, young pods, fresh shell seeds and dry seeds, preparation differences, and the caution against eating large amounts of raw pods. This was not generalized to common bean.

## Editorial changes

- Adzuki now distinguishes its dry-seed maturity cues from the species' food uses; no named variety, exact home harvest date, or cultivar-specific flavor was invented.
- Chickpea has separate dry-seed harvest context and cooked culinary uses; field maturity and food guidance remain at crop/species scope.
- Lentil culinary use now reflects the Vicia lens identity while explicitly preserving the Lens culinaris synonym used by agronomy sources. The existing Plant Profile harvest/problem validation gaps remain.
- Mung bean now distinguishes cooked dry seeds from sprouting as a separate food use; the content warns against treating garden seed as food-sprouting stock and does not claim a pathogen-control protocol.
- Pigeon pea now has evidence for the predominant dry-stage market harvest. Green-pod timing remains unresolved rather than inferred.
- Scarlet runner bean now distinguishes its edible flowers, young pods, fresh seeds and dry seeds, including its species-specific raw-pod caution.
