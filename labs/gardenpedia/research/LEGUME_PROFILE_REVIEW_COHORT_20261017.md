# Legume Plant Profile review cohort — 2026-10-17

## Review outcome

Reviewed seven seed-grown identities: adzuki bean, butterfly pea, chickpea,
lentil, mung bean, pigeon pea, and scarlet runner bean. Existing supported
content was retained. All seven remain **partial** because important scope or
system questions remain unresolved; none was marked complete solely because it
has several sections or an associated Seed Profile.

## Identity-specific findings

- **Adzuki bean (*Vigna angularis*)** — POWO and the Oregon State Columbia
  Basin guide distinguish the species; regional field emergence, timing, and
  mature-pod cues are not converted to home-garden spacing or dates. The current
  profile already includes bilingual culinary use and scoped mature harvest.
- **Butterfly pea (*Clitoria ternatea*)** — NC State supports a climbing vine,
  outdoor cultivation, seed propagation, and temperate annual treatment; UF/IFAS
  supports flowers for tea and food color. Added bilingual Harvest Use using
  only those flower-use claims. No medicinal claim or storage lifetime.
- **Chickpea (*Cicer arietinum*)** — retained cool-season crop and waterlogging
  context, and the distinction between green-vegetable and dry-pulse harvest.
  Regional field disease and maturity guidance is not turned into a universal
  household protocol.
- **Lentil (*Vicia lens*)** — preserved Kew's accepted name and the
  *Lens culinaris* synonym used in agronomy sources. Field equipment and disease
  guidance remains explicitly distinct from a home-garden recipe.
- **Mung bean (*Vigna radiata*)** — Arkansas Extension provides home-garden
  culture and dry-seed harvest context. Sprouting is treated as a food use, not
  as a mature-plant harvest protocol; nonuniform pod maturity is retained.
- **Pigeon pea (*Cajanus cajan*)** — retained warm-region and field-crop limits.
  An ICRISAT nursery method is not presented as a household germination
  schedule; Texas harvest timing is not generalized to all types or climates.
- **Scarlet runner bean (*Phaseolus coccineus*)** — Wisconsin Extension
  supports climbing growth, trellis management, edible flowers/pods/seeds, and
  preparation/safety distinctions. Named dwarf and tall cultivar dimensions are
  not generalized to the unnamed identity.

## Shared unresolved gaps

The seven profiles have no defensible hydroponic-system validation. For several
unspecified seed types, a stable mature size or controlled growth-habit value
cannot be assigned. Outdoor light guidance remains separate from indoor grow
light. These remain explicit unknowns rather than being inferred from field
spacing or plant size.

## Evidence/source-family review

Existing registry evidence used includes Kew POWO, UW–Madison Agronomy and
Extension, Oregon State Extension, North Carolina State Extension, UF/IFAS,
Iowa State Extension, USDA NRCS, Arkansas Extension, Texas A&M AgriLife, and
ICRISAT. Crop-production sources are used only at their stated field/crop
scope; no new API/provider data was used.

The EN/ES section guidance was checked for all seven identities. Every Plant
Profile section had a Spanish guidance counterpart in its translation record.
All referenced source IDs resolve under the catalog validator. No profile is
signed off complete by this review alone.
