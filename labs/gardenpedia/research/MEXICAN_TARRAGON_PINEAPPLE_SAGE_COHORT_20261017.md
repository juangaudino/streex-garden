# Mexican Tarragon + Pineapple Sage review — 2026-10-17

## Mexican tarragon (*Tagetes lucida*)

- Kept the identity at species/type scope and distinct from French tarragon
  (*Artemisia dracunculus*); no cultivar was assigned.
- Corrected the cultivation and light evidence references that had pointed to
  unrelated Illinois herbs and Utah water-culture sources. Both now resolve to
  UF/IFAS Mexican tarragon guidance.
- Added the UF/IFAS-reported 2–3 ft height as a regional horticultural
  description (60.96–91.44 cm), medium confidence; spread remains unknown.
- Added bilingual Harvest Use for aromatic leaves and flowers, with source-led
  flavor/use notes and no invented shelf life. UF/IFAS supports fresh use,
  reduced flavor with drying/high heat, freezing or vinegar storage, and edible
  flower use. The existing contact-sensitivity caution remains scoped.
- Kept full sun as outdoor exposure; it does not establish an indoor-light tier
  or hydroponic suitability.
- Status: **partial**. Hydroponic suitability and a stable spread value remain
  unknown; regional freeze response is not universalized.

## Pineapple sage (*Salvia elegans*)

- Kept the profile at accepted species scope, without assigning named
  selections discussed by sources.
- Added bilingual Harvest Use for leaves and flowers based on UW–Madison and NC
  State Extension. Flavor notes are attributed to edible flowers and variation
  in leaf aroma is stated rather than promised universally.
- Preserved the propagation distinction: UW–Madison allows seed propagation
  but recommends tip cuttings to retain cultivars; NC State advises against
  routine seed growing unless started indoors and planted out as an established
  plant. No single stronger protocol was invented.
- Did not map outdoor full sun to indoor grow-light need. Hydroponic suitability
  and representative species-wide mature dimensions remain unknown.
- Status: **partial**.
