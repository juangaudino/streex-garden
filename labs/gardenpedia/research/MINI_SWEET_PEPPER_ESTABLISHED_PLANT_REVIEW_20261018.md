# Mini Sweet Pepper — established-plant review (2026-10-18)

## Editorial disposition

Reviewed `mini-lunchbox-sweet-pepper` as a mini sweet/bell pepper type within
*Capsicum annuum*. The exact seed-lot cultivar remains unverified, so named
Johnny's Lunchbox dimensions and maturity estimates are presented only as
clearly labeled series context, never as facts about this identity. No mature
plant dimensions or exact cultivar traits were inferred. The Plant Profile now
focuses on established-plant moisture, harvest, fruit-set stressors, crop-level
hydroponic reference values, and recognizable pepper disorders.
Germination and seedling thinning were removed from Plant Profile; seed-stage
guidance remains in its separate Seed Profile. Exact purchase-listing evidence
was removed from the public source registry; private seed inventory was not
changed.

## Evidence and scope

- University of Minnesota Extension, “Growing peppers”: outdoor crop-level
  guidance for consistent soil moisture, avoiding overhead sprinkling, heat
  related flower drop, blossom-end rot, sunscald and bacterial spot. Minnesota
  climate context is not a cultivar calendar.
- University of Maryland Extension, “Poor Blossom and Fruit Set of Vegetables”:
  crop-level differential for poor pepper fruit set, including prolonged dry or
  wet soil, temperature extremes, excessive shade, excess nitrogen and hot, dry
  winds. The reported temperature ranges remain Maryland Extension guidance,
  not cultivar-specific or universal thresholds.
- Utah State University Extension, “Peppers in the Garden”: crop-level harvest
  context; green and fully colored fruit can both be harvested. No specific
  mini/Lunchbox cultivar timing is inferred.
- Johnny's Selected Seeds, “Lunchbox Pepper Mix”: named-series context only.
  Its figures do not transfer to the unresolved seed lot.
- Oklahoma State University Extension: pepper crop-level EC/pH reference only;
  not a cultivar prescription and not standalone proof of system compatibility.

## Remaining gaps

Exact seed-lot cultivar, cultivar-specific dimensions/support habit, and
mini-type-specific harvest timing remain unresolved. The profile is therefore
marked partial rather than treating missing cultivar facts as established.
