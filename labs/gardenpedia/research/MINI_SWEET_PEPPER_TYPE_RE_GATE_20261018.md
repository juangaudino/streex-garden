# Mini Sweet Bell Pepper — type-scope re-gate (2026-10-18)

## Decision

`mini-lunchbox-sweet-pepper` is published as an unnamed mini sweet bell-pepper
horticultural type within *Capsicum annuum*, not as the named Johnny's Lunchbox
series or a specific seed-lot cultivar. Kew POWO accepts *Capsicum annuum* in
Solanaceae. No cultivar, fixed plant dimensions, or type-specific maturity
estimate is assigned.

The profile now passes the Plant Profile gate at this declared type scope. The
remaining unknown seed-lot cultivar does not block general pepper-crop
cultivation guidance because cultivar-specific claims are excluded and
dimensions/timing remain explicitly unspecified.

## Preserved evidence boundaries

- Kew POWO source `powo-capsicum-annuum-500` supports the accepted species,
  genus, and family only.
- Existing UMN, UMD, USU, and OSU evidence remains at pepper crop/species scope
  for moisture, harvest, heat-related fruit set, common disorders, and the
  documented hydroponic reference range.
- `johnnys-lunchbox-pepper` remains named-series context where explicitly
  relevant; its size and timing do not transfer to this catalog type.
- Crop-level hydroponic evidence is not converted to a nutrient-engine mapping,
  device compatibility, or cultivar-specific target.

## Changes

- Added bilingual identity section and a taxonomy reconciliation record.
- Cleaned the scientific-name field to the accepted binomial and changed the
  display name to avoid implying the named Lunchbox series.
- Removed named-series maturity figures from the identity metrics; retained
  only the crop-level OSU range with an explicit scope note.
- Updated the 501-row editorial inventory from partial to complete after
  individual review. All other unresolved type-specific values remain unknown.
