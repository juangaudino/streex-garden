# Mizuna type profile gate — 2026-10-18

## Decision

The whole Plant Profile passes at the accepted species/crop-type scope. Kew and
USDA GRIN both support *Brassica rapa* for the catalog's Mizuna type while
using different infra-specific treatments; the Oregon State figure-caption
discrepancy remains disclosed and is not used to claim *B. juncea*. No named
cultivar is represented.

## Hydroponic evidence boundary

The 2025 University of Tennessee/NASA study directly tested
*Brassica rapa* subsp. *nipposinica* 'Red Hybrid' in a controlled environment,
including a short raft DWC production phase. This supports reporting that
specific study, not generalized hydroponic suitability for all Mizuna or any
Garden appliance. The Plant Profile now classifies the experiment as
source-backed evidence at its actual cultivar scope and keeps broad-type
hydroponic suitability `unknown`. Experimental light, EC, pH and nutrient
conditions are not presented as household recommendations.
