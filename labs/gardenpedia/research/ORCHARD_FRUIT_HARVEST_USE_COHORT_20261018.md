# Orchard fruit Harvest Use cohort — 2026-10-18

Added bilingual, source-backed Harvest Use records for six existing public
identities: European Pear, European Plum, Japanese Plum, Sour Cherry, Sweet
Cherry, and Hardy Kiwi. Plant Profiles, Seed Profiles, taxonomy, and
compatibility were not changed in this cohort.

## Editorial decisions

- **European pear:** USU distinguishes European pears, which are harvested
  mature and ripened off the tree, from Asian pears, which ripen on the tree.
  The 2–3 month cold-conditioning instruction is explicitly limited to the
  cited winter cultivars (Anjou, Comice, Bosc), not generalized to the species.
- **European and Japanese plums:** species identity/use is supported by the
  corresponding NC State Extension records. USU's general plum-crop advice
  supports firm-ripe taste checks, successive pickings, and cool/refrigerated
  handling; it is not presented as species- or cultivar-specific timing.
- **Sour and sweet cherries:** kept as separate records. USU's general harvest
  guidance supports color/flavor checks and prompt refrigeration; its
  preservation guide supports freezing choices for both types. No single
  cultivar flavor, date, or household shelf life is promised.
- **Hardy kiwi:** NC State confirms the edible, smooth-skinned fruit. USU's
  regional *Actinidia arguta* guidance supports uneven ripening, harvesting
  before frost, continued ripening after picking, and a cool-storage context
  up to three weeks for fruit picked just before full ripeness. This is labeled
  as source context, not a universal guarantee.

All titles, descriptions, steps, edible-part labels, and practical-use text are
present in EN and ES. Method source IDs resolve through public sources or the
profile's public evidence references. Tested preservation recipes and
shelf-stable processing instructions remain delegated to the cited institutional
source; none were invented here.
