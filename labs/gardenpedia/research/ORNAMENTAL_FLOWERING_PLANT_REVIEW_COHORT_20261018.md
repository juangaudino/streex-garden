# Ornamental flowering plant profile review — 2026-10-18

Extension records:

- Rose of Sharon — `Hibiscus syriacus`
- Scarlet Star — `Guzmania lingulata`
- Wishbone Flower — `Torenia fournieri`

## Editorial outcomes

- Rose of Sharon: added the species-page flowering pattern and short-lived
  individual bloom. Cultivar colors and single/double forms remain unassigned;
  landscape dimensions and the invasive warning remain regional and are not
  translated into container claims.
- Scarlet Star: added NC State's general 1–2 ft height/width range to the
  established-plant description. The dimensions remain a species-page range,
  not a named-cultivar promise or pod spacing. Epiphytic medium and rosette-cup
  watering are not represented as hydroponic nutrition.
- Wishbone Flower: added mature annual habit/dimension and long-season
  flowering details; expanded the soil-moisture/root-cooling advice. The violet
  reference does not identify a cultivar, and shade/container suitability is
  not taken as indoor-system evidence.

Each profile has aligned EN/ES section keys and source references. Harvest is
not a crop-use requirement for these ornamental records. Unknown compatibility
values remain unknown. No identity, Seed Profile, private record, or new source
was added.
