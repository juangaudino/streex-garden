# Outdoor-light and established-profile cohort — 2026-10-18

Four records were reviewed individually against the completion rubric. The
work targeted real mismatches between documented outdoor exposure and the
compatibility data, plus whole-record sign-off for two already enriched flower
profiles.

- **Asparagus (*Asparagus officinalis*)** — UMN's sunny-site guidance is now
  represented as species-level outdoor `full_sun`. It is not an indoor lamp
  tier. Perennial crown/fern care, harvest establishment and recovery, fresh
  use/storage, food-safety caveats, pests and disease cues were reviewed in
  English and Spanish. Hydroponics, indoor dimensions and pod spacing remain
  unknown; those gaps do not block the profile's central outdoor-bed guidance.
- **Rhubarb (*Rheum rhabarbarum*)** — UMN's all-day-sun site requirement is
  represented as species-level outdoor `full_sun`, not grow-light guidance.
  Large-crown care, watering, division, harvest maturity/technique, toxic-leaf
  safety, uses and regional problem cues were reviewed bilingually. Hydroponic
  fit and indoor spacing remain unknown and are not central to the profile.
- **Ivory Princess Calendula (*Calendula officinalis*)** — species-level
  `full_sun` evidence now points to NC State Extension and UMN, while the local
  UC Master Gardener report remains only a local type/color/cut-flower
  observation. No cultivar dimensions, food-grade status or system fit were
  inferred.
- **Signet Marigold (*Tagetes tenuifolia*)** — replaced broad flower-guide
  compatibility pointers with the direct NC State species source. Published
  8–30 in height and 9–30 in spread are represented as rounded broad outdoor
  species ranges (20–76 cm; 23–76 cm), not container guarantees. Annual versus
  perennial behavior remains climate-dependent. Edibility applies only to
  some named cultivars, not this unspecified signet type.

All four passed the individual Plant Profile gate; unknown hydroponic fit was
retained. No Seed Profile, Harvest Use, canonical identity, schema, UI, or
runtime behavior was changed. Inventory was updated only for these four rows.
