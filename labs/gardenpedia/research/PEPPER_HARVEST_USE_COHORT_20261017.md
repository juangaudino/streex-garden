# Capsicum Harvest Use cohort — 2026-10-17

## Scope

Added bilingual Harvest Use records for 11 existing identities: Anaheim, Ancho,
Banana, Cayenne, Cubanelle, Habanero, Jalapeño, Mini Bell, Poblano, Serrano,
and Shishito pepper. No plant identity, Seed Profile, or propagation disposition
was changed. Existing harvest/uses sections remain the cultivation-profile
content; Harvest Use adds post-harvest decisions and practical actions.

## Evidence and scope

- Iowa State Extension supports crop/type-level form, culinary use, maturity,
  and hot-pepper handling across common pepper types.
- UMN Extension supports mature-size harvest, clipping when fruit does not
  release easily, repeat harvest, and hot-pepper handling. Regional growing
  calendars and temperatures were not transferred into these use records.
- Oregon State University supports representative cultivar/type ripening and
  uses, including the Poblano-to-dried-Ancho relationship. Its commercial
  storage conditions were not presented as home shelf-life guarantees.
- Iowa State Horticulture Research Station supports Shishito-specific culinary
  uses and the caveat that occasional fruit may be hotter than usual.

## Editorial cautions retained

- Anaheim pungency grouping differs across extension references; the record
  leaves its heat level unresolved rather than assigning a single value.
- Fresh Poblano and dried Ancho uses remain separate; this does not claim every
  Poblano seed lot is a named Ancho cultivar or add a drying protocol.
- Mini Bell uses are supported at bell/crop scope; no mini-cultivar size,
  stuffing suitability, or mature color is inferred.
- Banana pepper entry represents the sweet type; it does not generalize to hot
  banana selections.
- Cayenne and other hot types have no assigned universal Scoville value.
- No new shelf-life duration, canning process, or preservation safety recipe was
  added. Ornamental Black Pearl was excluded from culinary Harvest Use.
