## Product-specific information added

- Gardyn identifies the Pink Petunia product as *Petunia × hybrida*; Kew's
  accepted name is *Petunia × atkinsiana*. Neither establishes a named cultivar
  or the formal horticultural group.
- Product-specific Medium Light Zone and day/night temperature context were
  added beside, not merged with, UMN Extension's outdoor sun/soil guidance.
- Gardyn describes about 7–10 inches of height and an approximate 1 ft product
  size, soft pink fragrant flowers, spreading before bloom and natural cascading
  habit. Those descriptions are not promoted to universal cultivar dimensions
  or a controlled compatibility habit.
- Established care now covers monthly root checks, trimming brown/overlong roots,
  optional Plant Belt support, deadheading, branch trimming while retaining at
  least one-third, new bud clusters on trimmed branches, and the product's
  expected sticky feel.
- Harvest/use distinguishes UMN's general cut-flower guidance from Gardyn's
  explicit warning that this product is not edible. No culinary use is inferred.
