# Polka Dot Begonia — individual Plant Profile review (2026-10-18)

Identity: *Begonia maculata* Raddi, accepted by Kew; no named cultivar is
represented. The common name may be used for hybrids in commerce, so cultivar
traits are not implied.

The profile now includes source-scoped identity/foliage, growth habit and
size uncertainty, container/houseplant cultivation, flowering, conditional
pruning, and practical pest/disease monitoring in EN and ES. The direct UF/IFAS
Santa Rosa County species sheet lists part shade/indirect sun (2–6 hours), a
moist well-drained medium, container use, and pest/airflow guidance. Its table
lists 2–3 ft height and 1–2 ft spread, while its prose says usually 1–2 ft and
occasionally up to 10 ft, partly discussing hybrid variation. The conflict is
recorded rather than collapsed into a single mature dimension; compatibility
height/spread remain unknown. Cane-type habit and UF/IFAS pruning guidance are
retained at horticultural-group/genus scope, not asserted as a cultivar
schedule. Ambient light is not mapped to indoor grow-light intensity.

No propagation protocol was added: the existing separate Propagation Guidance
record correctly says the genus page does not resolve a *B. maculata*-specific
method. No Seed Profile, Harvest Use, private data, or hydroponic claim was
added. No Garden adaptation is needed for the new profile sections.
