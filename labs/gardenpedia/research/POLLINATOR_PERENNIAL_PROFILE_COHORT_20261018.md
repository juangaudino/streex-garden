# Pollinator and ornamental perennial Plant Profile cohort — 2026-10-18

## Scope

Individual established-plant review of eight existing species-level Plant
Profiles. No identities, Seed Profiles, private data, schema or runtime code were
added or changed.

## Decisions and profile additions

- **Butterfly Weed — *Asclepias tuberosa***: added regional flowering timing,
  deadheading/follicle management, aphid and wet-soil disease signals, host
  caterpillar context, and ingestion/sap safety. Seed-start details remain in
  the Seed layer.
- **Blazing Star — *Liatris spicata***: added the conditional staking signal for
  taller stems and distinguished host moth larvae from an assumed pest event.
  Existing stem retention for nesting bees was preserved.
- **Blue False Indigo — *Baptisia australis***: added after-flowering pruning
  only when seed pods are unwanted or shaping is needed; preserved ornamental
  pod use and the deep-taproot limitation. Pest/disease language now reflects
  the source's “no major problems reported,” not immunity.
- **Cardinal Flower — *Lobelia cardinalis***: added bottom-up flowering,
  regional flowering period, root-zone moisture/mulch care, and slug/snail
  foliage damage. Queen Victoria traits remain excluded from the species claim.
- **Caucasian Pincushion — *Lomelosia caucasica***: corrected propagation from
  unsupported to source-backed seed propagation and spring division, without
  inventing germination parameters. Added flowering, deadheading, autumn
  cutback, wet-winter/heat cautions, and the source's reported pest/disease
  status. 'Butterfly Blue' flowering traits remain cultivar-scoped.
- **Sea Thrift — *Armeria maritima***: added regional flowering colors/timing
  and documented rock-garden, border and container uses. Subspecies and
  cultivar variation remain explicit.
- **Snow-in-Summer — *Cerastium tomentosum***: added recognizable flowering
  form/timing and practical spring pruning, deadheading, division and large-bed
  management; heat, humidity, drainage and spreading caveats were preserved.
- **Common Foxglove — *Digitalis purpurea***: clarified self-seeding
  management, optional removal after flowering, and conditional staking for
  tall varieties. Toxicity and named-cultivar boundaries remain explicit.

All new or revised Plant claims have resolving source IDs and corresponding
source scope/claim-kind metadata. Spanish guidance was updated from the same
reviewed claim set. No numeric indoor/hydroponic suitability was inferred.

## Editorial gate

All eight identities were individually reviewed for established-plant value,
normal development, management/use, meaningful risks, EN/ES parity, source
resolution and taxonomic scope. The catalog's unknown indoor/hydroponic
compatibility values remain unknown and do not convert outdoor landscape
guidance into system specifications. No new Seed-stage protocol was written.
