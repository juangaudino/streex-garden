# Radish Harvest Use cohort — 2026-10-17

## Scope and editorial result

Added bilingual Harvest Use records for four existing identities:

- Cherry Belle Radish (`cherry-belle-radish`)
- French Breakfast Radish (`french-breakfast-radish`)
- Watermelon Radish (`watermelon-radish`)
- Daikon Radish (`daikon-radish`)

The shared evidence supports fresh use, salads, and snack use at garden-radish
crop scope. Each record states that these are not cultivar/type-specific flavor
or recipe claims. Daikon's fresh-use note preserves UMN's distinction that it
is generally less spicy than garden radishes. Existing identity, cultivation,
harvest, and problem guidance was inspected and not replaced. No timing,
root dimensions, storage duration, hydroponic suitability, or cultivar-specific
culinary advantage was added.

## Source and evidence scope

- University of Minnesota Extension, [Growing radishes in home gardens](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-radishes): general garden-radish fresh/salad/snack uses, harvest at usable size, removing greens and washing roots, and avoiding overmaturity. It also distinguishes long-rooted daikon and describes it as generally milder. Minnesota seasonal dates, soil distances, and storage conditions are not turned into universal or cultivar-specific values.
- Johnny's [French Breakfast type and product category](https://www.johnnyseeds.com/vegetables/radishes/long-french-radishes/) directly supports its described fresh serving forms; the claim is scoped to the traditional type and not every seed strain.
- Burpee's [Cherry Belle product page](https://www.burpee.com/products/radish-cherry-belle-prod000870) lists crudités, salads, soups, and roasting for its product; those are explicitly kept as supplier/product claims.
- A previously cited UF/IFAS Watermelon Radish blog now redirects to a 404 page. Its profile claim was replaced with [UCCE Master Gardeners—Yolo County's Tried and True Vegetables](https://ucanr.edu/sites/default/files/2024-02/394612.pdf), a regional list describing one open-pollinated Watermelon radish selection. Its appearance and mild-sweet flavor are not generalized to every market type.
- Existing Cherry Belle, French Breakfast, and Daikon profile claims and their source scopes were preserved.
