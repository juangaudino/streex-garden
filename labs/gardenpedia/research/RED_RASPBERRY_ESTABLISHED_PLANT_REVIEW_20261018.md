# Red Raspberry established-plant review — 2026-10-18

## Disposition

`red-raspberry` passes the Plant Profile gate as the red raspberry crop type
within accepted species *Rubus idaeus*, not a named cultivar. A demonstrated
Spanish naming defect was corrected: the old summary called it *zarzamora*
(blackberry); it now says *frambuesa roja*.

## Reviewed and added

- Penn State's home-bramble source distinguishes red raspberry from other
  brambles, describes perennial roots/biennial canes, summer-bearing versus
  primocane-fruiting cycles, and red raspberry's root suckers. No single
  bearing schedule or pruning calendar is assigned without a cultivar.
- Penn State's home-garden guidance supports full sun and good drainage. This
  is outdoor crop guidance, not an indoor grow-light tier, hydroponic claim,
  or container guarantee.
- Harvest now has an observable cue (ripe berries detach readily), careful
  rolling rather than squeezing/pulling, shallow containers, frequent picking,
  and prompt refrigeration. Harvest Use separately covers fresh fruit and
  freezing; it does not promise a storage duration.
- Anthracnose is included only as a possible cane problem: young canes may show
  enlarging, sunken reddish-purple spots and later gray bark. The profile does
  not claim inevitable infection or cultivar resistance.
- EN/ES sections were checked in parallel. Existing taxonomy and identity were
  preserved.

## Evidence and limits

- Kew POWO `kew-rubus-idaeus-wave27`: accepted species identity.
- Penn State Extension `psu-brambles-home-fruit-wave27`: home-garden type and
  cane-cycle context.
- Penn State Extension `psu-brambles-harvest-postharvest-2026`: harvest and
  postharvest handling for bramble crop group.
- Penn State Extension `psu-bramble-anthracnose-home-2026`: anthracnose signs
  and sanitation; species/cultivar incidence is not inferred.
- Indoor light, hydroponic suitability, cultivar dimensions and pod spacing
  remain unknown. The maintenance guidance stays conditional on identified
  fruiting type. These unknowns are explicit and do not block a useful general
  outdoor crop-type profile.

No Seed Profile, private seed inventory, Garden X history, or runtime data was
changed.
