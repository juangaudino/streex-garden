# Rhizome herbs Plant Profile review — 2026-10-18

Scope: `ginger-zingiber-officinale` and `turmeric-curcuma-longa`. This is an
individual Plant Profile gate, not a Seed Profile or hydroponic compatibility
approval.

## Evidence reviewed

- Kew POWO accepts *Zingiber officinale* and *Curcuma longa*; *Curcuma domestica*
  is a synonym of *C. longa*.
- NC State Extension's species pages document established-plant habit, garden
  conditions, flowering/propagation, rhizome harvest and selected stress/pest
  concerns. Ginger's partial-shade garden guidance and turmeric's morning-sun /
  afternoon-shade guidance remain species-level outdoor advice.
- University of Vermont Extension's 2019 home-growing article covers both crops
  in containers. Its temperature, pot, moisture, light-transition and harvest
  details are retained as that home-container method, not universal setpoints.

## Changes and editorial decisions

- Expanded both profiles in EN/ES with the UVM adult container context, keeping
  outdoor recommendations and indoor-window guidance distinct.
- Added a flowering section to each profile. Ginger's rare/late container
  flowering is presented as normal variation; turmeric's reported sterile
  flowers and non-viable seed are stated at species scope.
- Ginger harvest now distinguishes selective rhizome harvest for a continuing
  clump from UVM's seasonal full lift. Turmeric uses foliage dieback as the cue;
  source-specific seasonal/month ranges are not universalized.
- Turmeric pest notes include NC State's limited observations about mites in
  dry soil and slugs/snails on young leaves. No resistance claim was added.
- Existing bilingual Harvest Use guidance remains separate from mature-plant
  care; no seed-germination instructions were copied into Plant Profiles.
- Both identities are rhizome-propagated in the represented catalog. No Seed
  Profile was added. Container suitability was not converted into hydroponic
  suitability. Hydroponic fit and normalized indoor/outdoor light remain
  unknown in compatibility data.
- No material source conflict was found: partial shade versus sunny-window
  guidance describes different growing contexts; turmeric's outdoor exposure
  is likewise kept separate from indoor lighting. No cultivar is represented.

## Gate

Both profiles pass the Plant Profile rubric for their represented species-level
culinary scope. Unresolved hydroponic compatibility and normalized light fields
remain explicit unknowns and are not represented as known values.
