# Rice and spring barley established-plant review — 2026-10-18

## Scope

Reviewed only `rice-oryza-sativa` and `spring-barley-hordeum-vulgare` in
Plant Profile wave 60. The edits enrich established-crop lifecycle, management
signals and harvest cues. They do not create or change Seed Profiles, private
records, compatibility claims, or a home-growing protocol unsupported by the
selected evidence.

## Evidence reviewed

- University of Minnesota Extension, [Spring barley growth and development
  guide](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/crop-production/small-grains/spring-barley-growth-and-development-guide): Minnesota six-row spring-barley field phenology; spring versus winter type; tillering and normal tiller mortality; stress-sensitive pollination; kernel maturity cues. No cultivar-specific household dimensions are assigned.
- North Carolina State University Cooperative Extension, [Upland Rice Provides a Niche Market for Growers in Union
  County](https://union.ces.ncsu.edu/union-county-agriculture/upland-rice-provides-a-niche-market-for-local-growers-in-union-county/): regional upland-rice example; distinguishes it from standing-water systems and identifies pollination as a critical irrigation stage. Not a universal species or household prescription.
- University of Arkansas System Division of Agriculture Cooperative Extension Service, [Arkansas Rice Production Handbook — MP192](https://www.uaex.uada.edu/publications/mp-192.aspx): commercial Arkansas growth-stage framework, harvest/postharvest chapter coverage, and sheath-blight lesion/spread context. No household pesticide or cultivar-resistance claim is made.
- Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew POWO and USDA ERS rice sector source already in the wave-60 registry: accepted species identity and commercial production context.
- Tamil Nadu Agricultural University Agritech Portal, [Transplanted Puddled Lowland Rice — Harvesting](https://agritech.tnau.ac.in/agriculture/agri_cropproduction_cereals_rice_tranpudlow_harvesting.html): field-system-specific straw-color and hard-dough maturity cues, followed by harvest, threshing and winnowing. Not a universal date or home-growing prescription.
- Food and Agriculture Organization, [Rice in Human Nutrition — Post-harvest Processing, Parboiling and Home Preparation](https://www.fao.org/4/t0567e/T0567E0h.htm): crop-level hand-threshing, drying and dehulling context; not a validated household food-safety or storage process.

## Editorial decisions

### Rice — `rice-oryza-sativa`

- Expanded identity, lifecycle, cultivation-system distinction, flowering,
  field harvest cues, post-harvest grain handling, and recognizable
  sheath-blight context in English and Spanish.
- Explicitly distinguishes upland from standing-water rice; neither is treated
  as proof of recirculating hydroponic suitability.
- Keeps cultivar/subspecies, household dimensions, timing, yield and a validated
  household processing protocol unknown. A new Harvest Use distinguishes
  TNAU's transplanted lowland maturity cues from FAO's crop-level hand
  threshing/dehulling context. Grocery-grain viability and household
  germination remain `needs_validation`; no Seed Profile is implied.
- Status: **partial**. Evidence now supports a useful crop-level lifecycle, but
  a suitable seed identity and defensible home-scale cultivation information
  remain material gaps.

### Spring Barley — `spring-barley-hordeum-vulgare`

- Expanded tiller development, normal versus stress-associated tiller loss,
  pollination sensitivity, and kernel/head maturity cues in English and Spanish.
- Kept Minnesota timing and production observations regional; commercial grain
  moisture is not represented as household storage guidance.
- Removed germination minimum/depth emphasis from established-plant care and
  retained only the seed-propagation mode in the Plant Profile.
- Does not assign two-row/six-row form, cultivar, malting quality, end use,
  household size, indoor light or hydroponic fit.
- Status: **partial**. Crop lifecycle is more useful, but cultivar/end-use and
  household-scale cultivation evidence remain unresolved.

## Validation observations

The two profiles remain distinct from the broad grain-crop category. Source IDs
are attached only to claims within each source's stated taxonomic and regional
scope. Both EN/ES translations were updated from the same reviewed claim set.
