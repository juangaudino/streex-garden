  performance and resistance labels remain supplier-scoped.
- `red-sail-lettuce` — named looseleaf variety; AAS identity/appearance and
  Johnny's harvest guidance remain within their respective source scopes.

Each profile has bilingual established-plant identity, growth, cultivation,
care, harvest and problem guidance, plus a separate bilingual Harvest Use
record. Source references resolve. The profiles preserve unknown hydroponic
suitability, dimensions or system spacing where evidence does not establish
them. Parris Island's succession-sowing recommendation remains marked as a
Garden adaptation rather than source text.

Harvest Use is the plant-use layer for this cohort; its separate presence is
why the structural matrix's `missing_plant_use_section` flag does not mean the
published knowledge is absent. Companion and visual-guide records are not
completion requirements under the rubric.

## Evidence checked

- University of Minnesota Extension supports lettuce-level shallow-root and
  moisture context, tipburn after a dry spell followed by renewed growth,
  heat/long-day bolting, and leaf versus whole-plant harvest. Those facts are
  not represented as cultivar-specific.
- Utah State University Extension describes cos/romaine heading and harvest
  stages and commercial handling/storage; commercial storage conditions are
  not offered as a household duration guarantee.
- Johnny's Parris Island product page supports the named product identity,
  upright medium-green oval leaves, and its separate baby/full-size maturity
  estimates. It does not establish indoor or hydroponic settings.
- Adaptive Seeds Pandero product page supports the supplier-listed red-romaine
  identity, dense red heads, maturity estimates, and its own slow-bolting,
  temperature-tolerance and downy-mildew-resistance statements. The resistance
  claim is not generalized and is not described as immunity.
- Johnny's Breen listing supports its compact red mini-romaine identity,
  product dimensions, hydroponic/greenhouse labels, maturity estimate and
  specified resistance ratings; these are not generalized to other romaine.
- Cornell Cooperative Extension's 2019 trial gives a single-season Bronze
  Arrow observation, not a durable cultivar performance or disease-resistance
  guarantee.
- Johnny's Muir listing and hydroponic variety materials support its Batavian
  identity, product-described mini/full head behavior, hydroponic label and
  listed resistance ratings. No nutrient target or system layout is inferred.
- All-America Selections supports Red Sails' named variety/ornamental edible
  description; Johnny's separately supports the product's harvest guidance.
- Kew's accepted *Lactuca sativa* identity supports species nomenclature;
  it does not resolve cultivar-level attributes.
