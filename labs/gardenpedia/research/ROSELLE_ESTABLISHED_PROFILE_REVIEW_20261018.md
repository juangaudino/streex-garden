# Roselle established-plant profile review — 2026-10-18

Identity: `roselle-sabdariffa-gossypiifolia` — Roselle / Flor de Jamaica.

## Scope and evidence

- Kew POWO currently accepts *Sabdariffa gossypiifolia* (published 2025) and lists *Hibiscus sabdariffa* as a synonym. NC State and UF/IFAS horticulture pages use that synonym; their roselle cultivation evidence is retained at the reconciled species/crop scope, not treated as cultivar-specific.
- NC State documents the edible subshrub, leaf-development change, full sun, short-day flowering, container or in-ground culture, and the risk that cool short seasons may not yield useful calyces.
- UF/IFAS Gardening Solutions adds established-plant drainage/water guidance, early-pruning effect, a useful calyx harvest cue (tender/plump, before browning), subsequent flower-bud development, culinary uses, frost risk and root-knot nematodes. October/November timing, roughly one-week freshness and pest pressure remain Florida-scoped.
- UF/IFAS Pasco County reports 4–7 ft height and 3–5 ft spread; a Santa Rosa County demonstration-garden sheet reports 2–3 ft for both. These are conflicting regional examples, not cultivar dimensions. Do not collapse them into a single mature-size value.

## Editorial decision

Expanded the existing Plant Profile in EN/ES and added a bilingual Harvest Use record for calyces and young leaves. The profile distinguishes the seed capsule from the fleshy calyx and keeps seed-starting protocol out of the established-plant content. The locally documented dates and fresh-storage period are not generalized; no tested recipe, shelf-stable preservation procedure, medical claim, or cultivar claim was added.

Compatibility remains honest: full sun is documented outdoor exposure; indoor grow-light need, hydroponic suitability, mature dimensions and hydroponic pod spacing remain unknown. Soil spacing is not converted to pod spacing. The unresolved hydroponic fit and conflicting regional size examples do not prevent a useful species-level outdoor profile; those unknowns remain explicit, and the profile passes the Plant Profile gate without cultivar, indoor-light or system claims.
