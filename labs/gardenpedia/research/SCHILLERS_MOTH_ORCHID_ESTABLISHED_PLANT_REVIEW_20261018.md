# Schiller's Moth Orchid established-plant review — 2026-10-18

Identity: `schillers-moth-orchid-phalaenopsis-schilleriana` — accepted species
*Phalaenopsis schilleriana*, not an unidentified commercial hybrid or named
cultivar.

## Editorial outcome

The Plant Profile now has eight bilingual, source-backed sections for the
established ornamental orchid: identity, growth, cultivation, care, flowering,
pruning, propagation, and problems. The profile reports the species rather than
copying care assumptions for generic retail hybrids. No Seed Profile is
appropriate for the represented mature ornamental identity; RHS says seed
propagation is laboratory-only and documents keiki as the practical vegetative
route.

## Scope and conflicts retained

- Kew accepts the species and confirms its Philippine range and epiphytic habit.
- NC State describes this species as a specialist greenhouse orchid; the
  American Orchid Society describes it as relatively tolerant for a species
  orchid. These are differing horticultural characterizations, not a reason to
  silently choose one. The profile includes both.
- NC State gives plant height of 6–12 inches; RHS lists maximum height of
  0.5–1 m and describes 90–100 cm flowering stems. The profile notes that the
  sources may be measuring foliage versus the inflorescence differently and
  assigns no single compatibility height. Mature-size compatibility remains
  unknown; broad RHS/NC State spread context is not a device footprint.
- RHS supplies a watering cue, bark medium, seasonal bloom context, pruning of
  spent spikes, and keiki guidance. AOS adds species-specific light tolerance
  context and a fall-night flower-initiation cue; its general Phalaenopsis
  watering and humidity guidance is labeled at genus/culture-sheet scope.
- NC State, RHS, and AOS pest lists are presented as reported risks, not
  diagnoses or disease-resistance claims. RHS seasonal timing remains UK
  context. Filtered natural light is not mapped to an indoor grow-light tier;
  epiphytic mounting is not hydroponic/system compatibility.
