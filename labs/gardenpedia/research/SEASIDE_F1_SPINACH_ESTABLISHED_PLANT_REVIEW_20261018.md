# Seaside F1 Spinach established-plant review — 2026-10-18

Identity: `seaside-f1-spinach` — *Spinacia oleracea*, named Seaside F1 hybrid.

## Editorial outcome

The Plant Profile now covers the established crop in EN/ES. The previous public
record mixed seed-stage germination and thinning with mature-plant content, and
its English summary was Spanish. Those issues were corrected. Germination,
sowing, thinning after emergence, and transplant checkpoints remain in the
existing Seed Profile. A personalized sentence about User Zero's seed emergence

  oleracea* F1 and supports the compact upright habit, dark-green somewhat
  spade-shaped leaves, extended baby-leaf window, slow bolting, harvest methods,
  and race-/level-specific downy-mildew resistance. Its 30-day maturity is
  explicitly measured from seeding, so it is not presented as time after plant
  establishment. No numeric mature dimensions are supplied.
- Utah State University Extension supports general Utah/outdoor spinach guidance
  on sunlight, soil, temperature, moisture stability, heat/long-day bolting,
  established leaf harvest, and recognizable aphid/slug signs. None of those
  crop-level facts is promoted to a Seaside-specific trait or indoor prescription.
- Oklahoma State University's hydroponic table lists spinach at EC 1.8–2.3
  mS/cm and pH 6.0–7.0. These remain crop-level reference values, not a
  cultivar-specific trial, dosage rule, device compatibility guarantee, or
  Nutrient Engine mapping.
