# Second ornamental-perennial Plant Profile cohort — 2026-10-18

## Scope

Individual established-plant review of Lamb's Ear, Globe Thistle, Clary Sage,
Wild Bergamot, Common Yarrow and Pink Turtlehead. Existing identity records,
private data boundaries and the Plant Profile contract were preserved.

## Editorial decisions

- **Lamb's Ear — *Stachys byzantina***: added summer flowering and the
  evidence-backed choice to remove flower stalks when foliage/compact habit is
  the goal; retained cultivar variation, spreading behavior and wet-leaf/
  humidity disease cautions.
- **Globe Thistle — *Echinops ritro***: added regional bloom cues, deadheading
  tradeoffs (rebloom versus self-seeding), young-head timing for dried
  arrangements, and conditional staking/pest/drainage signals.
- **Clary Sage — *Salvia sclarea***: added flowering range, post-bloom cutback
  that can encourage a second flush, and source-reported soil/fertility
  cautions. Fragrance/flavoring uses remain distinct from medicinal or
  essential-oil advice.
- **Wild Bergamot — *Monarda fistulosa***: documented regional variety
  variation without assigning one color, clarified airflow care for mildew,
  and retained shortened hollow stems for stem-nesting bees.
- **Common Yarrow — *Achillea millefolium***: added flowering recognition,
  clarified that deadheading reduces seed spread but not rhizome spread, and
  retained cut/dried use plus the source's contact-dermatitis caution.
- **Pink Turtlehead — *Chelone obliqua***: replaced an unsupported propagation
  gap with source-listed seed/division/cutting routes (no germination
  parameters), and added flowering, host-pollinator context, conditional
  staking and mildew cues.

No cultivar-specific traits were generalized. Outdoor light, soil and
landscape dimensions were not mapped to indoor or hydroponic compatibility.
Seed establishment parameters were not added to Plant Profiles.
