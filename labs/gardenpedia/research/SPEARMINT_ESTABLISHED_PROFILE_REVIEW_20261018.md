# Spearmint — established-plant review (2026-10-18)

Refocused the Plant Profile on the established *Mentha spicata* perennial.
Removed the germination metric/section and seedling thinning from the Plant
Profile; those belong in seed-to-plant guidance. Replaced the wider commercial
height note with NC State Extension's species-level outdoor range of about
1–2 ft (30–61 cm), explicitly not an indoor or hydroponic guarantee. Added
source-backed containment guidance for the rhizomatous growth habit and
clarified pruning as pinching established growing tips.

The existing Kew, NC State, Johnny's, USU and Home Grown source references remain
resolved and correctly scoped. The existing bilingual Harvest Use stays intact.
Hydroponic suitability and solution EC/pH remain unknown because the reviewed
sources do not establish a supported system match or nutrient target. No Seed
Profile or compatibility data was changed.

The record remains **partial** until system suitability/nutrient evidence or an
editorial decision that this unsupported system dimension is not material.
