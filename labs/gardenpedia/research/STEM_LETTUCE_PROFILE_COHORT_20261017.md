# Celtuce established-plant profile review — 2026-10-17

## `celtuce-lactuca-sativa-augustana`

- Kept the accepted identity at *Lactuca sativa* with the Augustana/stem-lettuce
  horticultural group; no separate species or named cultivar is asserted.
- Added an established-plant summary and guidance explaining that the edible
  stem is the primary crop objective, with leaves also used.
- Added the important development issue: UC Davis describes long-photoperiod
  sensitivity and premature bolting; the stem elongates and the crop may become
  bitter before harvest. This helps distinguish normal stem growth from an
  early transition that shortens the useful harvest window.
- Added UC Cooperative Extension's Central Sierra context that intense sun can
  hasten bolting there and partial sun or shade cloth may help. This remains
  regional outdoor guidance; it is not converted into an indoor-light
  requirement, system compatibility or pod spacing.
- Existing Harvest Use remains the primary culinary layer and was not
  duplicated. No universal stalk size, maturity date, recipe or yield is
  claimed.
- Seed Profile V0 exists and was not modified. Plant Profile wording now
  explicitly keeps seed-to-established-plant instructions in that separate
  profile.
- Hydroponic suitability, indoor light, exact spacing and comparable mature
  dimensions remain unknown. Inventory status is `partial`, not complete.
