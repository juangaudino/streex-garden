# Strawberry established-plant cohort — 2026-10-18

Profiles reviewed: `monterey-strawberry` and `white-carolina-pineberry`.

## Evidence and claim scope

- UMN Extension's home-garden strawberry guide supports outdoor crop-level
  conditions, crown/runner anatomy, pollination context and ripe-fruit harvest
  cues. Its Minnesota soil-garden advice is not converted to indoor lighting,
  hydroponic parameters or pod spacing.
- UMN's day-neutral strawberry production page supports seasonal runner removal
  when fruit production is the goal. This is production-context guidance, not a
  small-system instruction.
- UC Davis identifies Monterey as day-neutral. Its comparative cultivar trial
  supports relative runner production versus newer trial lines, not a numeric
  mature spread.
- UC IPM's California cultivar table reports Monterey as susceptible to powdery
  mildew and states that susceptibility depends on pathogen-favorable
  conditions. The profile does not treat the rating as a diagnosis or a promise
  of infection.
- Illinois Extension's 2026 pineberry article supports type-level outdoor care,
  fruit appearance, achene-based maturity cues, gentle harvest and refrigeration.
  None of those type-level claims is presented as a White Carolina-specific
  cultivar result.

## Material conflict retained

Illinois Extension describes pineberries as self-sterile and recommends a
compatible red-fruited cultivar. A follow-up review found a direct White
Carolina seller conflict: Ebert's says the listed plants need pollen from
another strawberry, while Raintree labels the selection self-fertile but still
recommends an everbearing pollination partner for optimal pollination. UMN and
UConn describe cultivated strawberries generally as self-fruitful; that crop-
level statement cannot settle the direct product-page disagreement. The profile
now cites both commercial claims, keeps pollination as `needs_validation`,
does not prescribe a fixed pollinator ratio, and remains pending. Ebert's also
reports numerous runners; mature-size ranges are attributed separately to the
two sellers and not asserted as verified canonical dimensions.

## Editorial and privacy boundary

Monterey's public Plant Profile had inherited a private purchase-specific
statement and an exact User Zero purchase-listing source. Those details were
removed from public translation/source artifacts. The separate private seed
inventory was not changed. Monterey's public profile now covers the plant
cultivar and its sourced cultivation lifecycle without exposing ownership or
purchase quantity.

White Carolina's public translation also contained the private bare-root
material description; it was removed. Seed-to-plant instructions were not added
to either Plant Profile.

## White Carolina identity follow-up — 2026-10-18

USPP34612P2 identifies White Carolina/White Pine as an unpatented, white-fruited
Fragaria × ananassa parent of QBC-1. This is primary evidence for historical
breeding-name use, but it neither registers White Carolina nor authenticates
current nursery stock as the same clone. The patent gives two different USDA PI
numbers in separate passages, so neither accession is assigned. White Carolina
is now **partial**, not pending: the public profile can describe the
supplier-listed identity with those boundaries while the contradictory
pollination claims, exact stock equivalence and runner behavior remain
unresolved.

## Pollination follow-up — 2026-10-19

Walters Gardens' direct White Carolina product profile recommends one
everbearing variety per five White Carolina plants to help pollination and
landscape performance. Illinois Extension's 2026 pineberry article recommends
a compatible red-fruited garden strawberry for many pineberry cultivars and
gives a different, type-level ratio. Raintree likewise recommends one
everbearing partner per five White Carolina plants even though its listing
calls the selection self-fertile. This makes the practical recommendation
stronger: a compatible partner may improve fruit set/performance. It does not
resolve whether an unauthenticated White Carolina stock strictly requires a
partner, nor justify applying either ratio universally. The public profile now
preserves the disagreement and presents nearby compatible pollination as a
Garden adaptation; the identity remains partial.
