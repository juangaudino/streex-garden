# Sunflower established-plant completion cohort — 2026-10-18

Reviewed the complete public Plant Profile, its Spanish translation, compatibility
profile, source references, scope overlays, and the separate Seed Profile boundary
for three existing sunflower identities. These edits do not modify Seed Profiles.

## Dispositions

- **Dwarf Sunflower (*Helianthus annuus* dwarf-type group) — complete.** Iowa
  State defines dwarf cultivars as 3 ft or shorter, describes container/limited-
  space use, and distinguishes cultivar-specific forms. Adult outdoor care and
  cut-flower cues remain at species/crop scope; the profile does not invent a
  group-wide spacing, fixed mature dimensions, pruning rule, indoor-light tier,
  or hydroponic fit. The existing unknown states are intentional, not missing
  editorial work.
- **Teddy Bear Sunflower (*Helianthus annuus*, named cultivar) — complete.**
  Iowa State supports the named cultivar, 2–3 ft height and 5–6 in double-yellow
  flowers; NC State independently describes the 2–3 ft, fluffy/pompom-like,
  golden-yellow double heads. Added NC State to the registry and the specific
  identity, growth and flowering claims that relied on it. General species care,
  cut-flower harvest and problems remain explicitly scoped. No pruning, support,
  indoor dimensions or hydroponic compatibility is inferred.
- **Lemon Queen Sunflower (annual seed-grown *Helianthus annuus* selection) —
  complete.** Retained the explicit distinction from the unrelated perennial
  *Helianthus* ‘Lemon Queen’. Illinois Extension supports the annual selection's
  branching habit, multiple lemon-yellow 4–5 in heads and 60–96 in height range;
  the range is presented as that source's report, not a guarantee. Removed an
  unsourced comparison to a seed supplier. Adult outdoor care and cut-flower
  harvest remain species-scoped; indoor/system fit and a local bloom calendar
  remain unknown.

For all three, EN and ES preserve the same identity, scope and uncertainty.
Harvest is described as ornamental cut-flower use in the Plant Profile; no
separate culinary Harvest Use record was added because edible use is not the
represented purpose. Seed-start instructions stay in the corresponding Seed
Profiles. These identities are not evidence for one another's dimensions or
compatibility.
