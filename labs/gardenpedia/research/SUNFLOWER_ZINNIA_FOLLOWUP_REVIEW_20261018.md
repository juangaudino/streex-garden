# Sunflower and zinnia Plant Profile follow-up — 2026-10-18

## Dispositions

- **American Giant Hybrid Sunflower — complete.** The exact Ferry-Morse product page identifies *Helianthus annuus*, reports 12–16 ft, large multi-headed flowers, full sun, and edible seed use. Iowa State's separately listed “American Giant” is not used as independent confirmation. Its giant-type support advice and mature-head cues are scoped as crop guidance. Harvest Use now explains visible maturity and drying before roasting. Hydroponic suitability remains unknown.
- **Autumn Beauty Mixed Colors Sunflower — complete.** The exact product page supports the 5–6 ft range, multi-branching form, color mix, full sun, and cut-flower/ornamental purpose. Iowa State supplies general outdoor care and visible seed-head maturity cues. The profile distinguishes deadheading display flowers from heads intentionally left to mature. No edible-seed claim is inferred for this product.
- **Lilliput Mixed Colors Zinnia — complete.** Ferry-Morse supports the 16–18 in mix-level height and miniature/bushy description. UMN Extension supplies crop-level outdoor care and powdery-mildew monitoring; Illinois Extension supplies fully-open morning cut-flower handling. Pollinator value remains a supplier claim, qualified by UMN's warning that pollen/nectar traits vary among cultivars. The existing edible-petal Harvest Use is scoped to correctly identified, pesticide-safe flowers. No hydroponic fit is inferred.
- **Jolly Jester Marigold — partial.** NC State supports *Tagetes patula* species-level culture, but trade listings using the Jolly Jester name conflict: Johnsons Seeds lists a ~75 cm French marigold, while the Frisian Flower assortment labels *T. patula nana* Jolly Jester at approximately 25 cm. Without evidence that either listing matches the represented packet selection, no cultivar dimensions or dwarf/tall habit are promoted. Species-level guidance and ornamental use remain available.
