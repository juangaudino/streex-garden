## Rosemary

- Added a taxonomy identity section: Kew POWO accepts *Salvia rosmarinus* in Lamiaceae; *Rosmarinus officinalis* is a synonym used by Maryland Extension. The names are represented as one identity.
- Added cautious indoor-container care from UMN's general herb guide, which includes rosemary among warm-season herbs: water indoor pots when the upper half-inch of soil dries and avoid persistently soggy soil. This remains group-level advice, not a rosemary-specific frequency or hydroponic rule.
- Retained Maryland's full-sun, well-drained-soil and regional winter-protection guidance with its Northeast limitation. Existing Harvest Use already covers culinary uses and preservation and was not duplicated.
