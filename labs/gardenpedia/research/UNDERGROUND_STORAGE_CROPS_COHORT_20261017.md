## Editorial boundary

Harvest Use adds supported post-harvest/food-use information and does not replace Plant Profile cultivation. Seed-stage propagation details were not copied into mature-plant sections. Bare-root, slips, corms, and rhizomes remain distinct from botanical seed profiles. Unknown hydroponic fit, missing cultivar data, and unresolved normalized light values remain unknown rather than inferred.

The salsify citation-scope correction is a demonstrated evidence repair. This cohort is not a catalog-wide audit and does not authorize marking the remaining catalog complete.
