# Gardenpedia taxonomy backbone

`reconciliation-v1.json` is a Labs-only reconciliation layer. It records
accepted names, synonyms, family/genus/species, cultivar or horticultural type,
confidence, conflicts, and source provenance without changing a published
Gardenpedia identity automatically.

External taxonomy services are research inputs. Garden X does not call Kew,
USDA, Trefle, or another taxonomy service at runtime.
